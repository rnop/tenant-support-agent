# Standard Residential Lease Terms — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**
Managed by Alder Grove Property Management

## 1. Scope of these terms

This document sets out the standard lease terms offered at Cedar Ridge House. It is a
template. It is not a signed agreement and it records no particular tenancy.

An executed lease may differ. Where a signed lease or an attached addendum provides
otherwise, the signed document controls. A resident who wants to know what their own lease
says should consult their signed copy or contact the office.

**Cedar Ridge is a detached single-family home let as a whole.** The terms here differ in
substance from those at the two Long Beach apartment properties managed by the same office,
because the property, the city, and the applicable rules are all different.

## 2. The premises

The premises are the whole of the detached house at 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, comprising
three bedrooms, two full bathrooms and one half bathroom, an attached two-car garage, a
driveway, and the front and rear yards.

There are no shared areas, no other units, and no other residents on the property.

The property is for residential use only. Occupancy is limited to the persons named in the
signed lease. Adding an occupant requires the owner's written consent.

## 3. Term

The initial term is stated in the signed lease and is ordinarily twelve months. Unless the
signed lease provides otherwise, the tenancy continues month to month once the initial term
expires.

Term details are recorded in the signed lease: `[            ]`.

## 4. Rent

Rent is due on the **1st day of each month**, in advance and without demand.

The monthly rent is stated in the signed lease: `[            ]`.

A payment is credited on the day it is received.

## 5. Grace period and late payment

**Cedar Ridge allows a grace period of three days.** Rent received on or before the **3rd
day of the month** is not treated as late. A late fee applies from the 4th.

The late fee is **$50**, charged once in any month in which rent is late.

California sets no statutory late fee and requires no grace period. Both figures are terms
of this lease and are specific to this property.

## 6. Security deposit

The security deposit is **not more than one month's rent**. The amount is recorded in the
signed lease: `[            ]`.

The deposit is not rent and may not be applied as the final month's rent.

Handling, itemization, and return are covered by the Security Deposit Policy. Deposits are
itemized and returned within 21 days after the tenancy ends.

## 7. Utilities

**The resident holds every utility account for this property in their own name** and pays
each provider directly:

- water
- sewer
- trash and recycling collection
- electricity
- natural gas

Nothing is billed through the office and nothing is allocated or submetered. This differs
from the Long Beach properties, where water and trash are billed through the office.

Accounts are opened effective on the lease start date and closed at the end of the tenancy.

## 8. Condition of the premises

The property is let in the condition existing at move-in, as recorded in the move-in
inspection and accompanying photographs.

The owner maintains the property in a habitable condition as required by California law.

## 9. Maintenance — owner

The owner is responsible for:

- the roof, exterior walls, and structure
- plumbing, electrical, and gas systems
- the water heater
- the heating and air conditioning system, including annual servicing
- the built-in appliances supplied with the property
- the garage door mechanism
- exterior painting
- irrigation system faults
- termite and structural pest treatment

## 10. Maintenance — resident

The resident is responsible for:

- **replacing HVAC filters** at least quarterly
- **replacing smoke and carbon monoxide detector batteries**, and reporting any detector
  that does not function
- replacing light bulbs, including exterior fixtures
- keeping drains clear of resident-introduced blockages
- resetting tripped breakers and the garbage disposal
- keeping gutters clear of leaf litter at ground-accessible points
- routine interior cleaning

## 11. Yard and exterior

**The resident maintains the yard.** This is a substantive difference from the apartment
properties, where the owner does it.

The resident is responsible for:

- mowing, edging, and watering the front and rear lawns
- trimming shrubs and hedges within reach from the ground
- weeding beds and hard surfaces
- operating the irrigation system on a reasonable schedule and reporting faults
- **placing refuse and recycling bins at the kerb on the collection day and returning them
  the same day**
- keeping the driveway and walkways clear and free of oil staining

The owner is responsible for tree work above ground-accessible height and for irrigation
system repairs.

The yard is to be kept to the standard recorded in the move-in photographs. Persistent
neglect is a breach of the lease.

## 12. Owner access

The owner or the owner's agent may enter for purposes permitted by California Civil Code
section 1954.

Except in an emergency, entry requires **at least 24 hours' written notice** stating the
date, approximate time, and purpose, and occurs during normal business hours.

No advance notice is required in an emergency.

Entry into the **yard** for landscaping inspection, irrigation repair, or tree work is
subject to the same notice requirement.

## 13. Alterations

Painting, fixture changes, and any alteration require the owner's written consent. This
includes wall anchors, mounted televisions, and changes to the yard such as removing
planting, laying hard surfacing, or installing structures.

## 14. Quiet enjoyment

The resident is entitled to quiet enjoyment of the property, and is responsible for their
own conduct and their guests' conduct in relation to the surrounding neighbourhood.

Quiet hours are **9:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.**

## 15. Animals

Pets are permitted subject to the Pet & Animal Policy, which sets the limits and the pet
deposit.

**Assistance animals are not pets.** Service animals and support animals are not subject to
the pet limits or the pet deposit in this lease or in the Pet & Animal Policy. Requests
involving an assistance animal are handled by the office.

## 16. Smoking

**Smoking is prohibited inside the house and inside the garage.**

Smoking is permitted outdoors on the property. Material must be fully extinguished and
disposed of safely, and not left in the yard or on the driveway.

## 17. Garage and driveway

The attached two-car garage and the driveway are for the resident's use.

The garage is for vehicles and household storage. It may not be used as living or sleeping
accommodation, as a workshop involving power tools outside the hours in the Home Rules, or
for any commercial activity.

Vehicles parked on the property must be operable and currently registered. Vehicle repair
beyond routine servicing is not permitted.

**There is no electric vehicle charging installed.** Fitting a charger is an alteration
requiring the owner's written consent.

## 18. Renters insurance

The resident maintains renters insurance with personal liability coverage of at least
**$300,000**, and provides proof at move-in and on renewal.

## 19. Guests

A guest may stay up to **14 consecutive days**. A longer stay requires the owner's written
consent and may require the guest to be added to the lease.

## 20. Ending the tenancy

A resident ending a month-to-month tenancy gives **30 days' written notice**.

An owner ending a month-to-month tenancy gives 30 days' written notice where the resident
has occupied less than one year, and 60 days' written notice at one year or more.

**This property is subject to the just cause requirements of the City of San Diego
Residential Tenant Protections Ordinance**, notwithstanding the statutory exemption
described in section 21. Under the city ordinance those protections apply **from the first
day of the tenancy** rather than after twelve months.

**Questions about ending a tenancy, about a notice a resident has received, or about
relocation assistance are directed to staff and are not answered from this document.**

## 21. Rent increases and the statutory exemption

**This property is exempt from the rent limits of California Civil Code section 1947.12.**

The statutory exemption notice required for that exemption appears in full in the Required
Disclosures Packet and in the AB 1482 Exemption Addendum, and is included in the signed
lease.

The exemption concerns the **rent cap**. It does not remove the just cause protections that
apply under the City of San Diego ordinance — see section 20.

Notice requirements for a rent increase still apply. See the Rent Increase & Renewal Policy.

## 22. Addenda and disclosures

The signed lease may include addenda that change or add to these terms. Addenda used at
Cedar Ridge include the **AB 1482 Exemption Addendum**, the Pet Addendum, the Mold &
Moisture Addendum, and the Bed Bug Addendum.

Required disclosures are in the Required Disclosures Packet.

**Cedar Ridge was built in 1998. The federal lead-based paint disclosure does not apply.**

A resident who wants to know which addenda are attached to their own lease should consult
their signed copy or contact the office.
