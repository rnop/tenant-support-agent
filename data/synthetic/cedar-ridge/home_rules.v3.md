# Home Rules & Resident Responsibilities — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**
Effective 1 August 2025. Supersedes version 2.

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to the resident, members of the household, and guests.

Cedar Ridge is a detached single-family home. There are no shared areas and no other units,
so these rules concern the resident's obligations for the property itself and its
relationship to the surrounding neighbourhood.

## 2. Quiet hours

**Quiet hours are 9:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m., every day.**

> Changed from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. under version 2, following the city's noise provisions
> and neighbourhood complaints about early-morning yard equipment.

These are earlier in the evening and later in the morning than at either Long Beach property.

During quiet hours, avoid amplified sound audible at the property line, power tools, and
yard equipment.

## 3. Noise and the neighbourhood

Outside quiet hours, keep noise to a level reasonable for a residential street.

Yard equipment — mowers, blowers, trimmers — is used only between **8:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m.**

Gatherings should not extend into the street or the neighbouring properties. Where a
gathering is likely to be large or run late, tell the neighbours in advance; it prevents most
complaints.

## 4. Refuse and recycling bins

The resident is responsible for bins.

- Bins go to the kerb **on collection day**, not the night before where avoidable.
- **Bins are returned from the kerb the same day** they are emptied.
- Bins are stored out of view from the street between collections — beside the garage or on
  the side return.
- Bins are not overfilled, and lids close.
- Bulky items are not left at the kerb. The city operates a bulky item collection service and
  arrangements are the resident's to make.
- Household hazardous waste is not placed in any bin.

Bins left at the kerb between collections are the most common cause of neighbourhood
complaints about this property.

## 5. Lawns and planting

The resident maintains the yard to the standard recorded in the move-in photographs:

- lawns mowed and edged
- beds weeded
- shrubs and hedges trimmed within reach from the ground
- planting watered, subject to section 6
- fallen leaves and debris cleared
- the property line kept clear of overgrowth encroaching on the pavement or a neighbour

The owner is responsible for tree work above ground-accessible height. **Do not attempt tree
work requiring a ladder** — report it.

## 6. Irrigation and water restrictions

The resident operates the irrigation system on a reasonable schedule.

**Where the city or the water authority imposes watering restrictions, those restrictions are
followed.** Planting lost to lawful restriction is not the resident's responsibility and is
not charged.

Irrigation faults — broken heads, leaking valves, a failed controller — are **reported to the
office, not repaired by the resident**. They are the owner's cost.

Planting lost because the system was not run, when it could lawfully have been run, is the
resident's responsibility.

## 7. Driveway and garage

- Vehicles parked on the property are operable and currently registered.
- Vehicle repair beyond routine servicing is not carried out on the property.
- Oil and fluid leaks are cleaned from the driveway promptly. Staining is deductible at
  move-out.
- The garage is for vehicles and household storage. It is **not** living or sleeping
  accommodation, a workshop outside the hours in section 3, or a place of business.
- The garage door is kept closed when the property is unattended.
- **There is no electric vehicle charging installed.** Fitting one is an alteration under
  section 10.

## 8. Smoking and fire risk

**Smoking is prohibited inside the house and inside the garage.** It is permitted outdoors on
the property.

This neighbourhood sits close to open canyon land. Discarded smoking material is a fire risk.

- Material is fully extinguished and disposed of in a closed container.
- Nothing is discarded in the yard, in beds, in vegetation, or over a fence.
- Open fires and fire pits are not permitted anywhere on the property.
- Charcoal grills are permitted on hard surfacing only, never on the lawn or within three
  metres of the house, fence, or vegetation, and are never left unattended.
- Dry brush and accumulated leaf litter are cleared, particularly at the rear boundary.

## 9. Guests

A guest may stay up to **14 consecutive days**. A longer stay requires the office's written
consent.

The resident is responsible for guests' conduct, including parking so as not to obstruct
neighbours' driveways and observing quiet hours.

## 10. Alterations to the yard

The following require the office's written consent: removing or replacing planting or turf,
laying hard surfacing, installing a structure such as a shed, playhouse, trampoline, or
pergola, altering fencing, installing lighting or an electric vehicle charger, and installing
a pool or spa of any size, including inflatable.

## 11. Absences

Where the property will be unoccupied for more than **14 consecutive days**, tell the office.

Arrange for the yard to be maintained and the bins to be managed during the absence. An
unmaintained yard and bins left at the kerb signal an empty house.

Do not shut off utilities during an absence. Loss of power or water can damage the property,
and the resident is responsible for damage that results.
