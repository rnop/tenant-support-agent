# Security Deposit Policy — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**
Effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. The limit

**The security deposit at Cedar Ridge is not more than one month's rent.**

This is the amount collected at this property. It does not vary with whether the house is
let furnished or unfurnished, and it does not vary with the number of occupants.

Where the resident is a service member, the one month limit applies in all cases.

## 2. The deposit is refundable

The deposit is a refundable sum held against unpaid rent and damage. It is not a fee, not a
cleaning charge, and no part of it is non-refundable.

It is not rent, and the resident may not direct that it be applied as the final month's
rent.

A separate refundable pet deposit may be held under the Pet & Animal Policy. Refundable
deposits held for the property together do not exceed the limit in section 1.

## 3. Condition record at move-in

Before the resident takes possession the office records the condition of the property and
takes **timestamped photographs** covering every room, every closet, the appliances, the
fixtures, the garage, **and the front and rear yards**.

The yard photographs matter more here than at an apartment, because the resident maintains
the yard under the lease and the move-in photographs establish the standard it is kept to.

The resident receives the record and the photographs. Disagreement with any part of it
should be put in writing within **five days** of move-in.

## 4. Requesting an inspection before move-out

The resident may request an inspection before the tenancy ends. It is optional and free.

The office gives at least **48 hours' written notice** unless the resident waives it in
writing. It ordinarily takes place in the final two weeks and the resident may attend.

The resident then receives an itemized statement of what would presently be deducted, with
time to put those items right.

## 5. Condition record at move-out

The office photographs the property once belongings are removed and **before any cleaning or
repair begins**, and again once that work is finished. All photographs are timestamped.

## 6. What may be deducted

- rent that remains unpaid
- cleaning the property back to the standard of cleanliness recorded at move-in
- repair of damage beyond ordinary wear caused by the resident, the household, or a guest
- restoring the yard to the condition recorded at move-in, where it has been allowed to
  deteriorate — see section 7
- replacing or restoring personal property, where the signed lease so provides

Where work has been carried out the statement includes the invoice or a good faith estimate;
where materials were purchased, the receipt.

## 7. The yard

The resident maintains the yard under the lease. Deductions relating to the yard are
assessed against the move-in photographs.

**Deductible:** overgrown lawns, unweeded beds, dead planting attributable to a failure to
water, accumulated refuse, oil staining on the driveway, and damage to the irrigation system
caused by the resident.

**Not deductible:** normal seasonal change, planting lost to drought restrictions or to
disease, tree work above ground-accessible height, and irrigation faults not caused by the
resident. Those are the owner's responsibility under the lease.

## 8. What may not be deducted

**Ordinary wear and tear is never deducted.** Carpet worn along traffic routes, paint faded
by sunlight, small nail holes, and appliances worn by normal use are ordinary wear.

No deduction is made for a condition already recorded in the move-in photographs.

## 9. The 21-day statement

Within **21 days** of the tenancy ending and the resident vacating, the office sends an
itemized statement of every deduction together with any balance owing, and the supporting
invoices, estimates, or receipts.

The 21 days run from the end of the tenancy, not from the date keys are returned where those
differ.

## 10. Method of refund

Where the resident paid rent electronically during the tenancy, the balance is refunded
electronically unless the resident and the office have agreed in writing on another method.

Otherwise a check is sent to the forwarding address the resident provides. The resident
should give the office a forwarding address before moving out.

## 11. The resident's own deposit

This document states the policy at this property. **It does not record the deposit held, the
progress of any refund, or the reason for any deduction.**

**A resident asking about their own deposit is directed to the resident portal or to the
office.**
