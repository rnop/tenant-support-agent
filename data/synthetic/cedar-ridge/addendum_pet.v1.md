# Pet Addendum (Template) — Cedar Ridge House

**This is a blank template. It is not a signed agreement and it records no actual tenancy.**

## 1. What this addendum is

Attached to a signed lease at Cedar Ridge House where the resident has been approved to keep
a pet. It records the animal and the terms.

**Where attached to a lease, this addendum's terms apply in addition to the Pet & Animal
Policy, and where the two differ, this addendum governs for that tenancy.** A resident should
check their own signed lease rather than rely on the general policy.

## 2. Not for assistance animals

**A service animal or a support animal is not a pet and is not recorded on this form.** No
pet deposit is charged for an assistance animal, and no weight or breed restriction applies
to one.

Assistance animals are handled by the office as a reasonable accommodation. Do not use this
form.

## 5. Conditions

The resident agrees that:

- the animal is leashed in the front yard, on the driveway, and anywhere off the property
- the animal is not left outside unattended overnight
- waste is picked up from the rear yard at least twice weekly and immediately elsewhere, and
  placed bagged in the refuse bin
- persistent barking or howling will be addressed, particularly during quiet hours
- the animal is not left in the yard barking while the property is unoccupied
- the resident is responsible for all damage caused by the animal
- vaccinations and any required licence are kept current

## 6. Yard and fencing conditions

- Gates are kept closed and latched.
- **The resident inspects the fence and gates and reports any gap, damage, or failed latch to
  the office.** An animal escaping through a fence the resident knew to be damaged and had
  not reported is the resident's responsibility.
- Digging damaging irrigation lines, planting, or lawn is repaired at the resident's cost.
- A dog run, kennel, or additional fencing requires the office's written consent as an
  alteration.

## 7. Withdrawal of approval

Approval may be withdrawn where the animal causes repeated disturbance to neighbours, damage,
or a threat to others' safety, following written notice and a reasonable opportunity to
resolve it.
