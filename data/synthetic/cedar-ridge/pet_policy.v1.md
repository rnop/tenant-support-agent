# Pet & Animal Policy — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**

## 1. Assistance animals are not pets

**A service animal or a support animal is not a pet, and nothing in the remainder of this
document applies to one.**

An assistance animal is outside the limits in section 3, the restrictions in section 4, and
the pet deposit in section 5. **No pet deposit is charged for an assistance animal**, and no
breed, weight, or size restriction is applied to one.

This holds whether the animal is a trained service animal or a support animal connected to a
disability.

**Requests involving an assistance animal go to the office.** They are a reasonable
accommodation matter rather than a pet approval, and they are not decided under this policy
or by automated assistance. A resident who needs an assistance animal, or has questions about
one, should contact the office directly.

The resident remains responsible for damage caused by an assistance animal and for the animal
not becoming a nuisance. That responsibility is separate from the deposit in section 5, which
does not apply.

## 2. Approval required

A pet needs the office's written approval before arriving at the property. Approval is
recorded in a Pet Addendum to the lease and covers a named animal; it does not transfer to a
replacement.

## 3. Number and weight

- Maximum **two pets**.
- **No weight limit** applies at this property.

The house has a fully fenced rear yard, which is why the weight limit that applies at Maple
Court does not apply here.

## 4. Types of animal

Permitted with approval: dogs, cats, caged birds, fish, and small caged animals such as
rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, and gerbils.

Not permitted: reptiles, amphibians, arachnids, ferrets, farm animals of any kind including
poultry, and any animal prohibited by San Diego municipal code or California law.

Beekeeping is not permitted.

## 5. Pet deposit

- **Pet deposit: $750**, refundable.
- **Pet rent: none.**

The deposit is higher here than at either Long Beach property because the resident has the
run of a house, a garage, and a yard rather than an apartment.

Refundable deposits held for this property together do not exceed one month's rent. See the
Security Deposit Policy, section 1.

The pet deposit is refundable except to the extent applied to pet-related damage under
section 9. It is not a non-refundable fee.

**No pet deposit applies to an assistance animal.** See section 1.

## 6. The yard and fencing

The rear yard is fully fenced and a dog may be off-leash within it.

- The resident checks the fence and gates and **reports any gap, damage, or failed latch to
  the office**. A dog escaping through a fence the resident knew was damaged is the
  resident's responsibility.
- Gates are kept closed and latched.
- **Animals are not left outside unattended overnight.**
- Digging that damages irrigation lines, planting, or the lawn is repaired at the resident's
  cost. See section 9.
- Dogs are leashed in the front yard, on the driveway, and anywhere off the property.

The resident may not install a dog run, kennel, or additional fencing without the office's
written consent. That is an alteration under the Home Rules, section 10.

## 7. Waste

Animal waste is picked up **from the rear yard at least twice weekly**, and immediately from
the front yard, the driveway, and the pavement.

Waste is bagged and placed in the refuse bin, never the recycling. It is not buried in the
yard, placed in a green waste bin, or left in the yard between collections.

Accumulated waste in the yard is deductible at move-out and is a common cause of neighbour
complaints.

## 8. Noise and the neighbourhood

A detached house on a residential street carries a different noise expectation from an
apartment. Persistent barking, particularly during the quiet hours in the Home Rules, is
audible to several neighbouring properties.

The resident is responsible for addressing persistent barking or howling. Repeated
complaints are a nuisance and are handled under the lease.

Dogs are not left in the yard barking while the property is unoccupied.

## 9. Damage

The resident is responsible for damage caused by an animal, including scratched doors and
frames, damaged flooring, chewed fixtures and skirting, soiled carpet, odour requiring
treatment, damage to the garage interior, **damage to fencing and gates**, and **damage to
lawn, planting, and irrigation caused by digging**.

Flea or pest treatment made necessary by an animal is charged to the resident.
