# Required Disclosures Packet — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**
Detached single-family home. Built 1998. Managed by Alder Grove Property Management.

## 1. About this packet

This packet contains the disclosures given to the resident at Cedar Ridge House when a lease
is signed. It describes conditions and policies at this property and contains no information
about any individual resident.

Cedar Ridge is in the City of San Diego. The two other properties managed by this office are
in Long Beach and are governed by different local rules. Nothing in the Long Beach documents
applies here.

## 2. Statutory exemption notice — rent cap and state just cause

The following notice is required by California Civil Code sections 1947.12 and 1946.2 and is
reproduced in the exact statutory wording. It appears in the signed lease and in the AB 1482
Exemption Addendum.

> This property is not subject to the rent limits imposed by Section 1947.12 of the Civil
> Code and is not subject to the just cause requirements of Section 1946.2 of the Civil
> Code. This property meets the requirements of Sections 1947.12(d)(5) and 1946.2(e)(8) of
> the Civil Code and the owner is not any of the following: (1) a real estate investment
> trust, as defined by Section 856 of the Internal Revenue Code; (2) a corporation; or (3) a
> limited liability company in which at least one member is a corporation.

This notice must be contained in the lease at the time it is signed. It cannot be added to a
tenancy afterwards.

## 3. What the exemption does not do

The notice in section 2 concerns the **state** rent cap and the **state** just cause
statute. It has a narrower effect than residents sometimes expect.

**The City of San Diego Residential Tenant Protections Ordinance applies to this property
regardless of the state exemption.** The ordinance extends just cause requirements to
tenancies that section 1946.2 would exempt, and under the city ordinance those protections
apply **from the first day of the tenancy** — not after twelve months.

So the position at this property is:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| State rent cap (Civ. Code 1947.12) | **Does not apply** |
| State just cause (Civ. Code 1946.2) | **Does not apply** |
| City of San Diego just cause (RTPO) | **Applies, from day one of the tenancy** |
| Notice periods for ending a tenancy (Civ. Code 1946.1) | Apply |
| Notice periods for a rent increase (Civ. Code 827) | Apply |

**Questions about termination of a tenancy, about a notice the resident has received, or
about relocation assistance are handled by staff.**

## 4. Bed bugs

Bed bugs are small wingless insects that feed on blood, usually at night. Signs include itchy
welts, small dark spots on bedding or furniture, shed skins, and live insects in mattress
seams, bed frames, and furniture joints.

The resident should report suspected bed bugs to the office promptly. Early reporting makes
treatment easier and less disruptive.

The owner does not show, rent, or re-rent the property while it is known to be currently
infested.

Where the property is inspected, the resident is told the results within two business days.

## 5. Mold

The owner is not aware of any mold at this property exceeding levels known to affect health,
other than mold visible on ordinary inspection.

Mold follows moisture. The resident can reduce the risk by running the bathroom and kitchen
fans, ventilating the house, wiping surfaces where condensation collects, and reporting
leaks and water intrusion promptly.

Reports of water intrusion are treated as urgent maintenance.

## 6. Megan's Law notice

Notice: Pursuant to Section 290.46 of the Penal Code, information about specified registered
sex offenders is made available to the public via an Internet Web site maintained by the
Department of Justice at www.meganslaw.ca.gov. Depending on an offender's criminal history,
this information will include either the address at which the offender resides or the
community of residence and ZIP Code in which the offender resides.

Neither the owner nor Alder Grove Property Management is obliged to consult this database
and neither has done so on the resident's behalf.

## 7. Methamphetamine and fentanyl contamination

The owner has received no order from a health official identifying this property as
contaminated by methamphetamine or fentanyl, and is unaware of any such contamination.

## 8. Smoking policy

**Smoking is prohibited inside the house and inside the attached garage.**

**Smoking is permitted outdoors on the property** — in the yards, on the driveway, and on the
patio.

The prohibition and the permission both cover cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookah, cannabis in
smoked or vaporized form, and electronic cigarettes and vaporizers.

Material must be fully extinguished and disposed of safely. In a canyon-adjacent
neighbourhood, discarded smoking material is a fire risk; it is not to be left in the yard,
on the driveway, or in vegetation.

## 9. Utilities

**Every utility at this property is held in the resident's own name** and paid directly to
the provider: water, sewer, trash and recycling collection, electricity, and natural gas.

Nothing is billed through the office. There is no allocation and no submetering at this
property, so the disclosures required under Civil Code section 1940.9 for shared or
allocated metering do not arise here.

The resident opens the accounts effective on the lease start date and closes them at the end
of the tenancy. Allowing an account to lapse can damage the property, and the resident is
responsible for damage that results.

## 10. Lead-based paint — not applicable

Cedar Ridge was constructed in **1998**. The federal lead-based paint disclosure applies to
housing built before 1978 and **does not apply to this property**. No lead-based paint
disclosure is issued for Cedar Ridge.

## 11. Asbestos — not applicable

Given the year of construction, no asbestos disclosure is issued for this property.

## 12. Flood hazard

The owner is not aware that this property lies within a special flood hazard area or an area
of potential flooding as designated by the applicable authorities.
