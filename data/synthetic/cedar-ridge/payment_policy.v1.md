# Payment & Billing Policy — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**
Effective 1 April 2025.

## 1. When rent is due

Rent is due on the **1st day of each month**, in advance and without demand.

A payment is credited on the day it is received, not the day it is sent.

## 2. Grace period

**Cedar Ridge allows a grace period of three days.**

Rent received on or before the **3rd day of the month** is not treated as late. Rent
received on or after the **4th** is late.

Where the 3rd falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the grace period runs to the end of
the next business day.

A grace period is a term of this property's lease. California law does not require one, and
the length differs between the properties this office manages.

## 3. Late fee

The late fee at Cedar Ridge is **$50**, a flat amount.

It is charged once in any month in which rent is late. It does not accrue daily and it does
not compound.

California fixes no maximum late fee by statute. The amount here is set by the lease as a
reasonable estimate of the administrative cost of processing a late payment.

## 4. Returned payments

Where a payment is returned unpaid by the resident's bank it is treated as never having been
made.

- First returned payment during a tenancy: **$25**
- Every subsequent returned payment: **$35**

A returned payment does not extend the due date or the grace period. Where the replacement
arrives on or after the 4th, the late fee in section 3 applies as well.

## 5. How to pay

Rent is paid through the resident portal by bank transfer or card, or by check or money
order delivered to the office. Cash is not accepted.

Autopay is available through the portal and is optional.

## 6. Partial payments

Cedar Ridge accepts a partial payment **only where an arrangement has been agreed in writing
with the office in advance**.

An unarranged partial payment is returned to the resident and the rent remains unpaid.

A resident who cannot pay the full amount by the 3rd should contact the office **before** the
due date.

## 7. Fees that are not charged

Cedar Ridge does not charge:

- a fee for serving or delivering a legal notice to the owner or the office
- a fee for paying the security deposit by check
- a fee for submitting a maintenance request
- a fee for an on-time rent payment by any accepted method

## 8. Utilities are not billed through the office

**No utility is billed through the office at this property.** Water, sewer, trash,
electricity, and gas are all held in the resident's own name and paid directly to each
provider.

There is no allocated charge, no submeter charge, and no utility line on the resident
statement. The statement shows rent and any fees only.

This differs from both Long Beach properties, where water and trash are billed through the
office.

A resident with a question about a utility bill should contact that provider. The office
does not hold those accounts and cannot see them.

## 9. Notices about unpaid rent

Where rent remains unpaid the office may serve a written notice. A notice to pay rent allows
three days, not counting Saturdays, Sundays, or judicial holidays.

**A resident who has received a notice should contact the office. Questions about a notice,
about what follows it, or about a resident's own balance are handled by staff and are not
answered from this document.**
