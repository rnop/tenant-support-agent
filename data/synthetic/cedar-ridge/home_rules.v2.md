# Home Rules & Resident Responsibilities — Cedar Ridge House

**Cedar Ridge House, 11482 Cypress Canyon Road, San Diego, CA 92131**
Effective 1 March 2022. Superseded 31 July 2025 by version 3.

> Retained for reference. This is not the current set of rules. Quiet hours changed in
> version 3, and version 3 added the fire risk provisions in its section 8 and the absence
> provisions in its section 11, neither of which appears here.

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to the resident, members of the household, and guests.

## 2. Quiet hours

Quiet hours are **10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.**, every day.

> Changed in version 3 to 9:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.

## 3. Refuse and recycling bins

Bins go to the kerb on collection day and are returned the same day. Bins are stored out of
view from the street between collections. Bulky items are not left at the kerb.

## 4. Lawns and planting

The resident maintains the yard — mowing, edging, watering, weeding, and trimming within
reach from the ground. Tree work above ground-accessible height is the owner's
responsibility.

Irrigation faults are reported to the office.

## 5. Driveway and garage

Vehicles parked on the property must be operable and registered. Vehicle repair beyond
routine servicing is not permitted. The garage is for vehicles and household storage and is
not living or sleeping accommodation.

## 6. Smoking

Smoking is prohibited inside the house and inside the garage. It is permitted outdoors on the
property, and material must be fully extinguished and disposed of safely.

> Version 3 expanded this section substantially with fire risk provisions, including rules on
> grills, open fires, and brush clearance. Those do not form part of this version.

## 7. Guests

A guest may stay up to 14 consecutive days. A longer stay requires the office's written
consent.

## 8. Alterations to the yard

Removing planting, laying hard surfacing, installing a structure, altering fencing, and
installing a pool or spa require the office's written consent.
