# Payment & Billing Policy — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 January 2022. Superseded 30 June 2024 by version 2.

> This version is retained for reference. It is not the current policy. Two provisions
> below — the grace period in section 2 and the deposit limit in section 6 — no longer
> reflect either this property's policy or current California law.

## 1. When rent is due

Rent is due on the **1st day of each month**, in advance, without demand.

## 2. Grace period

Rent received on or before the **5th day of the month** is not treated as late.

A late fee applies from the 6th.

## 3. Late fee

A late fee of **$50** applies to rent received on or after the 6th of the month, charged
once in any month in which rent is late.

## 4. Returned payments

- First returned payment in a tenancy: **$25**
- Each subsequent returned payment: **$35**

## 5. How to pay

Rent is paid through the resident portal, or by check or money order delivered to the
office. Cash is not accepted.

## 6. Security deposit limit

The security deposit may not exceed **two months' rent** for an unfurnished unit.

> Superseded. From 1 July 2024 the limit at this property is one month's rent. See the
> Security Deposit Policy.
