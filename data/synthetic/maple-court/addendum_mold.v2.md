## 1. What this addendum is

Mold grows indoors where moisture is present. Most indoor mold problems begin with a water
source — a leak, condensation, or humidity that is not ventilated away. Controlling
moisture is what controls mold.

This addendum sets out what each party does about moisture in the unit. It is attached to
every lease at Maple Court.

## 2. The owner's statement

The owner is **not aware of any mold in this unit or in the shared areas** that exceeds
levels known to affect health, other than mold that would be visible on ordinary
inspection.

The unit was inspected before the tenancy and any moisture damage found was repaired.

## 3. What the resident agrees to do

- Use the bathroom ventilation fan during and for at least 20 minutes after showering or
  bathing.
- Use the kitchen ventilation fan when cooking, particularly when boiling or steaming.
- Keep the unit reasonably ventilated. Open windows when weather allows.
- Wipe down surfaces where condensation collects, including window frames, sills, and
  bathroom tile and glass.
- Allow air to circulate — leave a gap between furniture and exterior walls, and do not
  pack closets against exterior walls.
- Dry spills, overflows, and tracked-in water promptly.
- Hang wet towels and clothing to dry rather than leaving them piled.
- Keep the unit's heating at a reasonable level in cold weather; unheated rooms condense.
- Not block or disable any ventilation fan or vent.

## 4. What the resident agrees to report

Promptly, to the office:

- any leak, drip, or water intrusion, however small
- any overflow from a bath, shower, sink, or toilet
- any damp patch, staining, bubbling paint, or swollen woodwork
- any visible mold growth
- a persistent musty or earthy smell
- a ventilation fan that does not work
- a window or door that admits rain

**Water intrusion is treated as urgent maintenance.** Reporting it early is the single most
effective thing a resident can do.

## 5. What the owner does

On receiving a report, the owner inspects, identifies and repairs the moisture source, and
remediates affected material.

Remediation of anything beyond a small surface area is performed by contractors experienced
in mold remediation. Because this building was constructed in 1962, work that disturbs
painted surfaces or floor tile also follows the precautions in the Lead-Based Paint
Disclosure and the asbestos disclosure.

## 6. Access for inspection and remediation

Inspection and remediation require access. Except in an emergency, the office gives at
least 24 hours' written notice. Substantial water intrusion is an emergency and does not
require advance notice.
