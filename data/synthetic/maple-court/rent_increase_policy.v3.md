# Rent Increase & Renewal Policy — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 August 2025. Superseded 31 July 2026 by version 4.

> This version is retained for reference. It states the limit for the cap year that ended
> 31 July 2026. It is not the current policy.

## 1. Coverage

This property is subject to the rent limits of California Civil Code section 1947.12.

## 2. The annual limit

The maximum increase in any twelve-month period is 5% plus the regional change in the
Consumer Price Index, and never more than 10%.

For the cap year **1 August 2025 through 31 July 2026**, the applicable maximum for
properties in Long Beach was **8.0%**.

## 3. How often rent may be increased

Rent may be increased no more than twice in any twelve-month period, and the increases
together may not exceed the annual limit.

## 4. Notice

An increase of 10% or less within any twelve-month period requires 30 days' written notice.
An increase of more than 10% requires 90 days' written notice.

## 5. Questions about a specific increase

This document does not state the rent for any unit or whether a particular unit's rent was
increased. Residents with questions about their own rent are directed to the resident
portal or to the office.
