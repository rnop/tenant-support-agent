# Move-In & Move-Out Procedures — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes version 2.

## 1. Before move-in

Before keys are released the office requires the signed lease and addenda, the security
deposit and first month's rent in cleared funds, and proof of renters insurance meeting the
minimum in the lease.

## 2. Move-in inspection and photographs

The office inspects the unit with the resident before possession, recording the condition
of each room, the appliances, the fixtures, the flooring, and the paint.

The office takes **timestamped photographs** of each room, each closet, the appliances, and
the fixtures at the same time.

The resident receives a copy of the inspection record and the photographs. A resident who
disagrees with anything in the record should say so in writing within **five days** of
move-in, and the record is amended or the disagreement noted.

This record is the reference point for any deduction at the end of the tenancy. It is worth
the resident's time.

## 3. Keys

Keys are released at the move-in appointment: unit key, mailbox key, laundry room key, and
where applicable a trash enclosure key.

Additional or replacement keys are obtained from the office. **Residents may not rekey or
add a lock without the office's written consent.**

## 4. Utilities at move-in

Electricity and gas are held in the resident's name and should be transferred to take
effect on the lease start date.

Water, sewer, and trash are billed by allocation through the office and require no action
by the resident. See the Required Disclosures Packet, section 8.

## 5. Moving day

Moving is permitted **8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.**, any day.

Walkways, stairs, and the courtyard must not be obstructed. Packing material is broken down
and removed by the resident; it is not left in or beside the trash enclosure.

A moving vehicle may use the parking area temporarily but may not block another unit's
assigned space. Where a large vehicle is expected, tell the office in advance.

## 6. Giving notice to move out

A resident ending a month-to-month tenancy gives **30 days' written notice**. Notice may be
delivered to the office or submitted through the portal.

Notice given part-way through a month runs 30 days from the date it is received, not to the
end of the calendar month, unless the signed lease says otherwise.

A resident leaving at the end of a fixed term should confirm their intention with the office
in writing.

## 7. The optional initial inspection

A resident may request an inspection before the tenancy ends. This is optional and it costs
nothing.

The office gives at least **48 hours' written notice** of the inspection unless the resident
agrees in writing to waive it. The inspection ordinarily takes place in the final two weeks.

The resident may be present. Afterwards the office provides an itemized statement of what
it would presently deduct, so the resident has the opportunity to remedy those items before
moving out.

## 8. Cleaning expected at move-out

The unit should be left as clean as it was at the start of the tenancy, as shown in the
move-in photographs.

That ordinarily means: appliances emptied and cleaned inside and out, cabinets and drawers
emptied and wiped, bathroom fixtures cleaned, floors swept and mopped or vacuumed, windows
and sills wiped, light fittings free of dust, and all personal property and rubbish removed.

Residents are not expected to repaint, to shampoo carpet that is merely worn, or to repair
ordinary wear.

## 9. Move-out photographs

The office photographs the unit after the resident's possessions are removed and **before
any cleaning or repair work begins**, and again after that work is complete. These
photographs are timestamped and support any deduction.

## 10. Returning keys

All keys are returned to the office by the end of the last day of the tenancy. Rent
continues to accrue while keys are held.

## 11. Deposit accounting

Within **21 days** of the end of the tenancy the office sends an itemized statement of
deductions with any remaining balance, together with invoices, estimates, or receipts
supporting the deductions.

Where the resident paid rent electronically, the refund is sent electronically unless the
resident and the office have agreed in writing on another method. Otherwise it is sent by
check.

## 12. Forwarding address

Residents should give the office a forwarding address before moving out. Where none is
given, the statement and any refund are sent to the last known address, which is the unit
being vacated.
