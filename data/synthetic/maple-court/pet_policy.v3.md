# Pet & Animal Policy — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**

## 1. Assistance animals are not pets

**A service animal or a support animal is not a pet, and nothing in the rest of this
document applies to one.**

An assistance animal is not subject to the pet limits in section 3, the restrictions in
section 4, or the pet deposit or pet rent in section 5. **No pet deposit and no pet rent is
charged for an assistance animal**, and no breed, weight, or size restriction is applied to
one.

This is the case whether the animal is a trained service animal or a support animal
prescribed in connection with a disability.

**Requests involving an assistance animal are handled by the office.** They are a
reasonable accommodation matter, not a pet approval, and they are not decided under this
policy or by automated assistance. A resident who needs an assistance animal, or who has
questions about one, should contact the office directly.

A resident remains responsible for damage caused by an assistance animal, and for the
animal not creating a nuisance. That is separate from the fees in section 5, which do not
apply.

## 2. Approval required

A pet requires the office's written approval before it comes to the property. Approval is
recorded in a Pet Addendum to the lease.

Approval is for a specific animal. It does not carry over to a replacement animal.

## 3. Number and weight

- Maximum **two pets** per unit.
- Maximum **45 pounds** per animal at full adult weight.

Where two animals are kept, both limits apply — two animals, each within the weight limit.

## 4. Types of animal

Permitted with approval: dogs, cats, caged birds, fish in tanks of 20 gallons or less, and
small caged animals such as rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, and gerbils.

Not permitted: reptiles, amphibians, arachnids, ferrets, farm animals of any kind, and any
animal prohibited by Long Beach municipal code or by California law.

Aquariums over 20 gallons require separate written consent because of the weight and water
risk in a building of this age.

## 5. Pet deposit and pet rent

- **Pet deposit: $500**, refundable, held in addition to the security deposit but subject
  to the same one month total limit on refundable deposits at this property.
- **Pet rent: $50 per month**, per unit, not per animal.

The pet deposit is refundable to the extent it is not applied to pet-related damage under
section 8. It is not a non-refundable pet fee.

**Neither charge applies to an assistance animal.** See section 1.

## 6. Conduct and control

Dogs are leashed at all times outside the unit, including in the courtyard, the parking
area, and on the walkways. Cats are not permitted to roam the property.

Animals are not left unattended on a balcony or patio, or tethered anywhere on the
property.

Persistent barking, howling, or noise that disturbs another unit is a nuisance and is
handled under the Building Rules.

Animals are not permitted in the laundry room.

## 7. Waste

Animal waste is picked up immediately, bagged, and placed in the trash enclosure. This
applies in the courtyard, the parking area, and on the walkways and adjacent pavement.

Litter is not disposed of in a toilet. Litter and waste bags go in the black bins, not the
recycling.

## 8. Damage

Residents are responsible for damage caused by an animal, including scratched doors and
frames, damaged flooring, chewed fixtures, soiled carpet, and odour requiring treatment.

Flea or pest treatment required because of an animal is charged to the resident.

## 9. Visiting animals

An animal brought to the property by a guest is subject to this policy for the duration of
the visit, including the leash and waste rules. A visiting animal does not require approval
or fees for a stay of a single day, but a stay beyond that is treated as a pet under
section 2.
