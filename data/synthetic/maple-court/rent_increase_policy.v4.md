# Rent Increase & Renewal Policy — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 August 2026. Supersedes version 3.

## 1. Coverage

This property is subject to the rent limits of California Civil Code section 1947.12. It is
a four-unit building constructed in 1962 and no exemption applies to it.

## 2. The annual limit

The maximum increase in any twelve-month period is **5% plus the regional change in the
Consumer Price Index, and never more than 10%**.

For the cap year beginning **1 August 2026**, the applicable maximum for properties in Los
Angeles County, which includes Long Beach, is **8.7%**.

For the previous cap year — 1 August 2025 through 31 July 2026 — the maximum was 8.0%. See
version 3 of this policy.

The limit is a ceiling, not a scheduled increase. It does not mean rent rises by that
amount, and it does not mean rent rises at all in a given year.

## 3. How often rent may be increased

Rent may be increased no more than **twice in any twelve-month period**, and the increases
together may not exceed the annual limit in section 2.

## 4. Notice

A rent increase takes effect only after written notice.

- An increase of **10% or less** within any twelve-month period requires **30 days'**
  written notice.
- An increase of **more than 10%** within any twelve-month period requires **90 days'**
  written notice.

Because the maximum permitted increase at this property is below 10%, increases here are
normally subject to the 30-day notice.

Notice states the new rent amount and the date it takes effect. Notice is delivered in
writing and is also posted to the resident portal.

## 5. Renewal

At the end of a fixed term the office contacts the resident about renewal, ordinarily 60 to
90 days before the term ends.

A resident who does not sign a new fixed term continues month to month on the same terms,
unless those terms are changed by a notice given under section 4.

Renewal is not automatic in the sense of committing either party to a further fixed term.
The tenancy continuing month to month is the default where nothing else is signed.

## 6. Questions about a specific increase

This document states the limits and the notice rules that apply at this property. **It does
not state the rent for any unit, whether a particular unit's rent is being increased, by how
much, or when.**

A resident asking about an increase to their own rent — the amount, the effective date, or
whether a notice they received is correct — is directed to the notice itself, to the
resident portal, or to the office.
