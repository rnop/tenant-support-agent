# Payment & Billing Policy — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 July 2024. Superseded 31 March 2025 by version 3.

> This version is retained for reference. It is not the current policy.

## 1. When rent is due

Rent is due on the **1st day of each month**, in advance, without demand. A payment is
credited on the date it is received.

## 2. Grace period

Maple Court does not offer a grace period. Rent not received by the end of the day on the
1st is late on the 2nd.

The five-day grace period offered under version 1 of this policy, which ran to the 5th of
the month, ended on 30 June 2024.

## 3. Late fee

A late fee of **$50** applies to rent received after the 1st, charged once in any month in
which rent is late.

## 4. Returned payments

- First returned payment in a tenancy: **$25**
- Each subsequent returned payment: **$35**

A returned payment does not extend the due date.

## 5. How to pay

Rent is paid through the resident portal by bank transfer or card, or by check or money
order delivered to the office. Cash is not accepted. Autopay is optional.

## 6. Partial payments

Maple Court accepts partial payments. A partial payment does not settle the month's rent
and does not stop a late fee from applying to the unpaid balance.

Payments are applied oldest balance first.

## 7. Administrative fees

- Delivery of a legal notice to the office: **$10**
- Security deposit paid by check: **$5** handling

> These two fees were discontinued on 1 April 2025 and are prohibited under current
> policy. See version 3, section 8.

## 8. Security deposit limit

The security deposit may not exceed **one month's rent**, effective 1 July 2024.

This replaced the two-month limit stated in version 1 of this policy.
