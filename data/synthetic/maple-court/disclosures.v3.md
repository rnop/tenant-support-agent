# Required Disclosures Packet — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Built 1962. Four units. Managed by Alder Grove Property Management.

## 1. About this packet

This packet contains the disclosures provided to every resident at Maple Court at the time
a lease is signed. It describes conditions and policies at the property. It does not
contain information about any individual resident or unit.

## 2. Bed bugs

Bed bugs are small wingless insects that feed on the blood of humans and animals. Common
signs of an infestation include itchy welts on the skin, small dark spots on bedding or
furniture, shed skins, and live insects in mattress seams, bed frames, or furniture joints.

Residents are asked to report suspected bed bugs to the office promptly. Early reporting
makes treatment substantially easier and less disruptive.

The office does not show, rent, or re-rent a unit that is known to be currently infested.

Residents will be notified of the results of any inspection of their unit within two
business days of the inspection. Where common areas are inspected, all residents are
notified of the results within two business days.

Residents are asked to cooperate with treatment, which normally requires preparing the unit
by laundering fabrics and providing access.

## 3. Mold

The owner is not aware of any mold in the units or common areas at this property that
exceeds levels known to affect health, other than mold that would be visible on ordinary
inspection.

Mold grows where moisture accumulates. Residents can reduce the risk by using the bathroom
and kitchen ventilation fans, keeping the unit reasonably ventilated, wiping down surfaces
where condensation collects, and reporting leaks, drips, and water intrusion promptly.

Residents are asked to report visible mold growth, persistent damp, or a musty smell to the
office. Reports of water intrusion are treated as urgent maintenance.

## 4. Asbestos

Maple Court was constructed in 1962. Buildings of this era may contain asbestos-containing
materials in floor tile, tile adhesive, ceiling texture, pipe insulation, and joint
compound.

Materials in the building that are known or presumed to contain asbestos are in good
condition and are not friable. Undisturbed asbestos-containing material in good condition
is not a hazard.

**Residents must not sand, scrape, drill, cut, or otherwise disturb floor tile, ceiling
texture, or pipe insulation.** Any work of that kind is performed by the owner using
trained contractors.

Residents who notice damaged or crumbling floor tile, ceiling texture, or pipe insulation
should report it to the office and avoid the area.

## 5. Megan's Law notice

Notice: Pursuant to Section 290.46 of the Penal Code, information about specified
registered sex offenders is made available to the public via an Internet Web site
maintained by the Department of Justice at www.meganslaw.ca.gov. Depending on an offender's
criminal history, this information will include either the address at which the offender
resides or the community of residence and ZIP Code in which the offender resides.

Neither the owner nor Alder Grove Property Management is required to check this database
and neither has done so on any resident's behalf.

## 6. Methamphetamine and fentanyl contamination

The owner has not received an order from a health official identifying this property, or
any unit in it, as contaminated by methamphetamine or fentanyl, and is not aware of any
such contamination.

## 7. Smoking policy

**Maple Court is a smoke-free property.** Smoking is prohibited in all areas, without
exception:

- inside every unit
- on balconies, patios, and porches
- in the courtyard
- in the laundry room and the trash enclosure
- in the parking area and on the walkways

The prohibition covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookah and waterpipe, cannabis in smoked
or vaporized form, and electronic cigarettes and vaporizers of every kind.

This policy applies to residents, members of the household, and guests.

## 8. Utility allocation

Water, sewer, and trash service at Maple Court are **not separately metered by unit**. The
building was constructed in 1962 with a single service connection for these utilities.

These services are billed to the property and allocated among the four units. **The
allocation is by unit, in equal shares — each unit is billed one quarter of the property's
water, sewer, and trash charges for the billing period.** The allocation does not vary with
occupancy, square footage, or measured consumption, because consumption is not measured per
unit.

The amount charged to the units is the amount the property is billed by the utility
provider. Nothing is added.

A resident may request a copy of the underlying utility bill for any period in which they
were charged. Requests are made to the office and are provided at no charge.

Allocated utility charges appear as a separate line on the resident statement and are
billed one month in arrears.

Electricity and gas are separately metered and are held in the resident's own name with the
provider. They are not billed through the office and are not part of the allocation.

## 9. Rent cap and just cause coverage

**This property is subject to** the rent limits of California Civil Code section 1947.12
and the just cause requirements of California Civil Code section 1946.2. It is also subject
to Long Beach Municipal Code chapter 8.99.

The applicable annual rent increase limit and the notice required are described in the Rent
Increase & Renewal Policy.

**Questions about termination of a tenancy, about a notice a resident has received, or
about relocation assistance are handled by staff.**

## 10. Lead-based paint

Maple Court was constructed before 1978. The federally required lead-based paint disclosure
is provided as a separate document, together with the pamphlet *Protect Your Family From
Lead In Your Home*. See the Lead-Based Paint Disclosure.

## 11. Flood hazard

The owner is not aware that this property is located within a special flood hazard area or
an area of potential flooding as designated by the applicable authorities.
