# Building Rules & Shared Areas — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 June 2025. Four units.

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to residents, members of the household, and guests. They cover the shared
areas of the building and conduct that affects the other units.

## 2. Quiet hours

**Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m., every day.**

During quiet hours, sound should not be audible from inside another unit. This applies to
televisions and music systems, musical instruments, power tools, appliances such as washing
machines where portable units are permitted, and gatherings.

## 3. Noise generally

Outside quiet hours, residents are asked to keep noise at a level reasonable for a
four-unit building with shared walls.

Work involving power tools, drilling, or hammering is limited to **9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.**

## 4. Laundry room

The laundry room is on the ground floor and contains **coin-operated** washing and drying
machines shared by the four units.

- Open **7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.**
- Machines are used on a first-come basis. There is no reservation system.
- Remove laundry promptly at the end of a cycle. Laundry left in a machine may be removed
  by another resident and placed on the folding surface.
- Clean the lint filter after each use.
- Do not leave the room unattended with a machine door propped open.
- Report a machine fault to the office. Do not attempt a repair.

Personal property left in the laundry room is not stored there and may be removed.

## 5. Courtyard

The courtyard is shared by the four units. It is available from **8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.**

Furniture provided in the courtyard stays in the courtyard. Personal furniture, planters,
and equipment may not be left there without the office's consent.

Charcoal and open-flame grills are not permitted anywhere on the property. Portable gas
grills are not permitted in the courtyard.

## 6. Trash and recycling

The trash enclosure is at the rear of the property, accessible from the parking area.

- Household rubbish goes in the black bins, bagged and closed.
- Recycling goes in the blue bins, loose and rinsed. No bagged recycling.
- Break down cardboard boxes flat.
- Bulky items — furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics — are **not** left in or
  beside the enclosure. Contact the office to arrange bulky item collection.
- Household hazardous waste, including paint, solvents, batteries, and fluorescent tubes,
  is not accepted. The city operates collection points for these.

The trash enclosure is not storage.

## 7. Parking

The property has **four parking spaces, one assigned per unit.** The assignment is recorded
in the signed lease.

- Park only in the assigned space.
- **There is no visitor parking.** Guests park on the street, subject to city rules.
- **There is no electric vehicle charging** at this property, and vehicles may not be
  charged from a unit's power or from a shared outlet.
- Vehicles must be operable and currently registered.
- Vehicle repair and maintenance, other than adding fluids or changing a tyre, is not
  permitted on the property.
- A vehicle parked in another unit's space or blocking access may be towed at the owner's
  expense.

## 8. Guests

A guest may stay up to **14 consecutive days**. A longer stay requires the office's written
consent.

Residents are responsible for their guests' conduct and for making guests aware of these
rules, particularly quiet hours and parking.

## 9. Smoking

**Maple Court is smoke-free throughout**, including inside units, on balconies and patios,
in the courtyard, in the laundry room and trash enclosure, and in the parking area.

This covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookah, cannabis in smoked or vaporized form, and
electronic cigarettes and vaporizers.

## 10. Balconies, patios, and walkways

Balconies and patios are not storage. Items that may be kept there: outdoor furniture,
plants in stable containers, and a bicycle where it does not obstruct passage.

Walkways, stairs, and landings must be kept clear. Nothing is stored on them at any time.

Nothing may be hung from balcony railings, including laundry, rugs, and towels.

Satellite dishes, antennas, and air conditioning units may not be installed without the
office's written consent.

## 11. Bicycles and storage

Bicycles may be kept inside a unit or on a balcony or patio where they do not obstruct
passage. They may not be kept in the laundry room, the courtyard, walkways, or stairwells.

**There is no storage unit, storage locker, or shared storage area at this property.**

## 12. Concerns about a neighbour

Residents with a concern about noise or conduct in another unit are asked to contact the
office rather than confront a neighbour directly. Reports may be made in confidence.

**Where a concern involves safety, harassment, or threatening behaviour, contact the office
directly and, where there is immediate danger, emergency services. Concerns of that kind
are handled by staff.**
