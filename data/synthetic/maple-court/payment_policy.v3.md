# Payment & Billing Policy — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 April 2025. Supersedes version 2.

## 1. When rent is due

Rent is due on the **1st day of each month**, in advance, without demand.

A payment is credited on the date it is received, not the date it is sent or postmarked.
A payment submitted after the office closes on the 1st is received on the following
business day.

## 2. Grace period

**Maple Court does not offer a grace period.** Rent not received by the end of the day on
the 1st is late on the 2nd.

California law does not require a grace period on residential rent. Where a property
offers one it is a term of that property's lease, not a statutory entitlement, and it
differs from property to property.

## 3. Late fee

A late fee of **$75** applies to rent received after the 1st.

The fee is charged once in any month in which rent is late. It does not accrue daily and
it does not compound.

California does not set a maximum late fee by statute. The amount stated here is set by the
lease as a reasonable estimate of the administrative cost of processing a late payment.

## 4. Returned payments

A payment returned unpaid by the resident's bank is treated as though it had not been made.

- First returned payment in a tenancy: **$25**
- Each subsequent returned payment: **$35**

A returned payment does not extend the due date. If the replacement payment arrives after
the 1st, the late fee in section 3 also applies.

After a second returned payment, the office may require that future rent be paid by a
method that does not depend on a bank clearing the payment.

## 5. How to pay

Rent is paid through the resident portal by bank transfer or card, or by check or money
order delivered to the office.

Cash is not accepted.

Autopay is available through the portal and is optional. A resident who enrols in autopay
remains responsible for confirming that the payment was drawn.

## 6. Partial payments

Maple Court **accepts partial payments**.

A partial payment does not settle the month's rent and does not stop a late fee from
applying to the unpaid balance. Accepting a partial payment does not waive any right of
the owner.

A resident who cannot pay the full amount on time should contact the office before the 1st.

## 7. How payments are applied

Payments are applied **oldest balance first**: outstanding fees and charges from prior
months, then prior months' rent, then the current month's rent.

A resident who wants a payment applied differently should contact the office in writing
before submitting it.

## 8. Fees that are not charged

Maple Court does not charge:

- a fee for serving or delivering a legal notice to the owner or the office
- a fee for paying the security deposit by check
- a fee for making a maintenance request
- a fee for a rent payment made on time by any accepted method

## 9. Utility charges

Water, sewer, and trash are billed by allocation and appear as a separate line on the
resident's statement. Electricity and gas are held in the resident's name and are not
billed through the office.

The allocation formula, the billing cycle, and the resident's right to request the
underlying utility bills are set out in the Required Disclosures Packet and the Utility
Allocation Addendum.

Utility charges are not rent. A dispute about a utility charge does not excuse payment of
rent.

## 10. Notices about unpaid rent

Where rent remains unpaid, the office may serve a written notice. A notice to pay rent
allows three days, not counting Saturdays, Sundays, or judicial holidays.

**A resident who has received a notice should contact the office. Questions about a notice,
about what happens next, or about a resident's own balance are handled by staff and are not
answered from this document.**
