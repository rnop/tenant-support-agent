# Pet Addendum (Template) — Maple Court

**This is a blank template. It is not a signed agreement and it records no actual tenancy.**

## 1. What this addendum is

This addendum is attached to a signed lease at Maple Court where the resident has been
approved to keep a pet. It records which animal is approved and on what terms.

Where this addendum is attached to a lease, **its terms apply in addition to the Pet &
Animal Policy, and where the two differ, this addendum governs for that tenancy.** That is
why a resident should check their own signed lease rather than rely on the general policy.

## 2. This addendum does not apply to assistance animals

**A service animal or a support animal is not a pet and is not recorded on this addendum.**
No pet deposit and no pet rent is charged for an assistance animal, and no weight or breed
restriction applies to one.

An assistance animal is handled by the office as a reasonable accommodation matter. Do not
use this form for one.

The standard amounts at this property are a $500 refundable pet deposit and $50 per month
in pet rent, charged per unit rather than per animal. The amounts recorded above are the
amounts that apply to this tenancy.

The pet deposit is refundable except to the extent it is applied to pet-related damage. It
is not a non-refundable fee.

## 5. Conditions

The resident agrees that:

- the animal will be leashed at all times outside the unit
- the animal will not be left unattended on a balcony or patio, or tethered on the property
- waste will be picked up immediately and placed in the trash enclosure
- the animal will not be permitted in the laundry room
- persistent noise disturbing another unit will be addressed
- the resident is responsible for all damage caused by the animal
- vaccinations and any required licence will be kept current

## 6. Withdrawal of approval

Approval may be withdrawn where the animal causes repeated disturbance, damage, or a threat
to the safety of others, after written notice and a reasonable opportunity to resolve the
problem.
