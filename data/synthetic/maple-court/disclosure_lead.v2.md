# Lead-Based Paint Disclosure — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Constructed 1962.

## 1. Why this disclosure exists

Federal law requires that residents of housing built before 1978 receive a lead-based paint
disclosure and an informational pamphlet before the lease takes effect.

Maple Court was constructed in **1962** and is therefore covered. Of the three properties
managed by Alder Grove Property Management, this is the only one built before 1978.

## 2. Lead warning statement

Housing built before 1978 may contain lead-based paint. Lead from paint, paint chips, and
dust can pose health hazards if not managed properly. Lead exposure is especially harmful
to young children and pregnant women. Before renting pre-1978 housing, lessors must
disclose the presence of known lead-based paint and lead-based paint hazards in the
dwelling. Lessees must also receive a federally approved pamphlet on lead poisoning
prevention.

## 3. What the owner knows

The owner has **no knowledge of lead-based paint or lead-based paint hazards** in the
units or common areas at this property.

The owner has **no reports or records** pertaining to lead-based paint or lead-based paint
hazards at this property.

The absence of records is not a finding that the property is free of lead-based paint. A
building of this age may contain lead-based paint beneath later layers of paint.

## 4. Records provided to residents

Where the owner later obtains any report, assessment, or record concerning lead-based paint
at this property, it is provided to residents.

## 5. Pamphlet

Every resident receives a copy of the federally approved pamphlet *Protect Your Family From
Lead In Your Home* before the lease takes effect. A replacement copy is available from the
office at no charge.

## 6. Renovation and repair work

Work that disturbs painted surfaces at this property is performed by contractors certified
for renovation in pre-1978 housing, using lead-safe work practices, including containment
of the work area and specialised cleanup.

Residents receive advance notice of work of this kind.

**Residents must not sand, scrape, burn, or dry-scrape painted surfaces.** Requests to
paint or to repair a painted surface go to the office.

## 7. What residents should do

Residents are asked to report deteriorating paint — peeling, chipping, chalking, cracking,
or damaged paint — to the office promptly. Deteriorating paint in pre-1978 housing is
treated as urgent maintenance.

Residents can reduce risk by keeping painted surfaces clean and free of dust, wiping window
sills and wells regularly, and preventing children from chewing painted surfaces.

Families with young children may wish to consult a physician about blood lead testing.
Questions about testing are a medical matter and are outside what the office can advise on.
