# Just Cause & Relocation Notice — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**

## 1. Read this first

**This document is provided for reference. It is not a substitute for advice about a
particular situation.**

A resident who has received a notice, who has been asked to move, or who has a question
about their own tenancy ending should contact the office directly and, if they wish,
independent legal assistance. Staff handle these matters. They are not addressed by
automated assistance and they are not decided from this document.

Nothing here states whether any particular notice is valid, whether any particular reason
qualifies, or what any resident is entitled to.

## 2. Coverage

This property is subject to the just cause requirements of California Civil Code section
1946.2 and to Long Beach Municipal Code chapter 8.99.

Under both, the protections apply once a resident has lawfully occupied the unit for
**twelve continuous months**. Where an adult was added to the tenancy later, different
timing rules can apply.

## 3. Just cause

Where the protections apply, a tenancy may be terminated only for a reason recognised by
the ordinance and the statute. Recognised reasons fall into two groups.

**At-fault reasons** relate to the conduct of the tenancy — for example non-payment of
rent, a breach of a material term of the lease, or a nuisance. Where the breach is curable,
the resident is given an opportunity to cure before a termination notice is served.

**No-fault reasons** do not involve any conduct by the resident. They include the owner or
a qualifying family member moving in, withdrawal of the property from the rental market,
substantial remodel, and compliance with a government order.

Relocation assistance is owed for no-fault terminations. It is not owed for at-fault
terminations.

## 4. Relocation assistance

Where a tenancy at this property ends for a no-fault reason, the resident is entitled to
relocation assistance of **one month's rent**.

The assistance is provided either as a direct payment, made before or at the time the
notice to vacate is served, or as a written waiver of the final month's rent. The choice of
method is the owner's.

Long Beach requirements can exceed the state minimum in particular circumstances, including
for a substantial remodel.

**What is owed in any particular case depends on the facts of that case. This section does
not determine any resident's entitlement.**

## 5. Where to get help

- Alder Grove Property Management — the office, during business hours
- City of Long Beach, tenant and landlord resources, Housing & Neighborhood Services
- Legal aid organisations serving Long Beach provide free advice to residents who qualify

A resident who has received any written notice is encouraged to keep it, note the date it
was received, and bring it to whichever of these they contact.
