# Smoke-Free Addendum (Template) — Maple Court

**This is a blank template. It is not a signed agreement and it records no actual tenancy.**

## 1. What this addendum is

California Civil Code section 1947.5 requires that a lease which prohibits smoking say so,
and identify the areas where smoking is prohibited. This addendum is attached to every
lease at Maple Court for that purpose.

## 2. The policy

**Maple Court is a smoke-free property.** Smoking is prohibited everywhere on the property,
including inside the units.

This is a condition of every tenancy at this property. It is not an option a resident
elects.

## 3. Where it applies

- inside every unit, including all rooms, closets, and private bathrooms
- on every balcony, patio, and porch
- in the courtyard
- in the laundry room
- in and around the trash enclosure
- in the parking area, including inside a parked vehicle
- on all walkways, stairs, landings, and entryways
- anywhere else on the grounds

## 4. What is covered

Smoking means inhaling, exhaling, burning, carrying, or holding any lighted or heated
product intended for inhalation, including:

- cigarettes and cigarillos
- cigars
- pipes and hookah or waterpipe
- cannabis in any smoked or vaporized form
- electronic cigarettes, vape pens, and any electronic device producing an inhaled vapour
  or aerosol

## 5. Guests

The resident is responsible for informing guests of this policy and for guests observing
it while on the property.

## 6. Breach

Smoking on the property is a breach of the lease.

The resident is responsible for the cost of remediating smoke odour and residue in the
unit, which in a building of this age can include sealing and repainting and replacing
soft furnishings supplied with the unit.

## 7. What the owner does not promise

The owner does not promise that the property is or will remain free of smoke drift. This
addendum states a rule and the owner's intention to enforce it; it is not a guarantee about
air quality.

A resident affected by smoke reaching their unit should report it to the office.
