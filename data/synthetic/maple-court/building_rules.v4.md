# Building Rules & Shared Areas — Maple Court

**Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**

## 1. Scope and effective date

**These rules take effect on 1 October 2026.** Until that date the current rules are
version 3.

The only substantive change from version 3 is to laundry room hours, in section 4.

These rules apply to residents, members of the household, and guests.

## 2. Quiet hours

**Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m., every day.** Unchanged from version 3.

During quiet hours, sound should not be audible from inside another unit.

## 3. Noise generally

Outside quiet hours, residents are asked to keep noise at a level reasonable for a
four-unit building with shared walls. Work involving power tools, drilling, or hammering is
limited to 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.

## 4. Laundry room

The laundry room is on the ground floor and contains coin-operated washing and drying
machines shared by the four units.

- **Open 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.** — changed from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. under version 3,
  following complaints about noise transmitting to the ground-floor units early and late.
- Machines are used on a first-come basis. There is no reservation system.
- Remove laundry promptly at the end of a cycle.
- Clean the lint filter after each use.
- Report a machine fault to the office. Do not attempt a repair.

## 5. Courtyard

Shared by the four units, available 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Furniture provided stays in the
courtyard. Charcoal, open-flame, and portable gas grills are not permitted.

## 6. Trash and recycling

Household rubbish in the black bins, bagged and closed. Recycling in the blue bins, loose
and rinsed. Cardboard broken down flat. Bulky items arranged through the office. Household
hazardous waste is not accepted.

## 7. Parking

Four spaces, one assigned per unit. Park only in the assigned space. There is no visitor
parking and no electric vehicle charging. Vehicles must be operable and registered. Vehicle
repair on the property is not permitted.

## 8. Guests

A guest may stay up to 14 consecutive days. A longer stay requires the office's written
consent. Residents are responsible for their guests' conduct.

## 9. Smoking

Maple Court is smoke-free throughout, including inside units, on balconies and patios, in
the courtyard, in the laundry room and trash enclosure, and in the parking area. This
covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookah, cannabis in smoked or vaporized form, and
electronic cigarettes and vaporizers.

## 10. Balconies, patios, and walkways

Balconies and patios are not storage. Walkways, stairs, and landings are kept clear at all
times. Nothing is hung from balcony railings.

## 11. Bicycles and storage

Bicycles may be kept inside a unit or on a balcony or patio where they do not obstruct
passage. There is no storage unit, storage locker, or shared storage area at this property.

## 12. Concerns about a neighbour

Residents with a concern about noise or conduct in another unit are asked to contact the
office rather than confront a neighbour directly.

Where a concern involves safety, harassment, or threatening behaviour, contact the office
directly and, where there is immediate danger, emergency services. Concerns of that kind
are handled by staff.
