# Property Fact Sheet — Maple Court

## 1. The property

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **Maple Court** |
| Address | **3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814** |
| Units | 4 |
| Built | 1962 |
| Type | Two-storey apartment building |

Maple Court is on the **west side of Roswell Avenue**. Harborview Apartments, also managed
by Alder Grove, is directly across the street at 3145 Roswell Avenue. **The two are
separate properties with separate rules, separate policies, and separate documents.**

## 2. Management office

Alder Grove Property Management

- **Office hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Closed weekends and public
  holidays.
- **Telephone:** (562) 555-0142
- **Email:** office@aldergrovepm.example
- Walk-in visits are welcome during office hours. Appointments are preferred for anything
  needing time.

The office serves both Roswell Avenue properties. When contacting the office, say which
property you are calling about.

## 3. Emergency maintenance

**24-hour maintenance line: (562) 555-0199**

Answered at all hours, including nights, weekends, and public holidays. Use this line for
gas odour, smoke or fire, flooding, no heat, carbon monoxide, no water, an electrical
hazard, a sewage backup, or a unit left unsecured.

**Where there is immediate danger to people, contact emergency services on 911 first.**

Non-emergency requests go through the resident portal, not this line. See the Maintenance &
Repair Policy.

## 4. Resident portal

**portal.aldergrovepm.example**

The portal is where residents pay rent, set up autopay, submit and track maintenance
requests, view their own statements and balance, view notices, and upload proof of renters
insurance.

**Anything specific to a resident's own tenancy — balance, payment history, request status,
lease documents, notices — is in the portal.** The office can help with access.

## 5. On-site

- Shared laundry room, ground floor, coin-operated
- Courtyard
- Trash and recycling enclosure at the rear
- Four assigned parking spaces, one per unit
- Mailboxes at the front entrance

## 6. What is not at this property

So that residents are not sent looking: Maple Court has **no** pool, gym, clubhouse,
package room or package acceptance, storage units, visitor parking, electric vehicle
charging, or on-site staff.

Standard Residential Lease Terms · Required Disclosures Packet · Lead-Based Paint
Disclosure · Payment & Billing Policy · Security Deposit Policy · Rent Increase & Renewal
Policy · Just Cause & Relocation Notice · Maintenance & Repair Policy · Building Rules &
Shared Areas · Move-In & Move-Out Procedures · Pet & Animal Policy

Copies are available from the office and in the portal.
