# Rent Increase & Renewal Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 August 2025. Superseded 31 July 2026 by version 6.

> Retained for reference. This version states the limit for the cap year that ended on
> **31 July 2026**. It is not the current policy. For the cap year beginning 1 August 2026
> see version 6.

## 1. Coverage

Harborview is subject to the rent limits in California Civil Code section 1947.12.

## 2. The limit for this cap year

For the cap year **1 August 2025 through 31 July 2026**, the maximum increase over any
twelve-month period at this property was **8.0%**.

The statutory formula is 5% plus the regional change in the Consumer Price Index, capped at
10%.

## 3. Frequency

Rent may be raised no more than twice in any twelve-month period, and the increases together
must stay within the limit in section 2.

## 4. Notice

30 days' written notice where the combined increase over the preceding twelve months is 10%
or less; 90 days' where it exceeds 10%.

## 5. A resident's own rent

This document does not state the rent for any unit or whether a unit's rent was increased.
Residents asking about their own rent are directed to the resident portal or to the office.
