# Building Rules & Shared Areas — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 March 2025. Four units.

## 1. Scope

These rules bind residents, members of the household, and guests. They govern the shared
areas and conduct affecting the other units.

## 2. Quiet hours

**Quiet hours run from 11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m., every day.**

During quiet hours sound should not carry into another unit. This covers televisions and
audio systems, instruments, power tools, appliances, and gatherings.

## 3. Noise generally

Outside quiet hours, keep noise to a level reasonable for a four-unit building with shared
walls and floors.

Work with power tools, drills, or hammers is confined to **9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.**

Residents in upper units are asked to be mindful of impact noise carrying to the unit below.

## 4. Laundry room

The laundry room is on the ground floor with **card-operated** washers and dryers shared by
the four units. Laundry cards are issued by the office and topped up in the portal.

- Open **6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.**
- First-come basis. There is no booking system.
- Take laundry out promptly at the end of a cycle. Items left in a machine may be moved to
  the folding surface by another resident.
- Clean the lint filter after every use.
- Report faults to the office. Do not attempt repairs.

Property left in the laundry room is not in storage and may be removed.

## 5. Rear patio

The rear patio is shared by the four units and is available **8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.**

Furniture provided stays on the patio. Personal furniture and equipment may not be left
there without the office's consent.

Charcoal and open-flame grills are not permitted anywhere on the property. **One shared gas
grill is provided on the patio**; residents using it clean it afterwards and shut off the
gas.

Smoking is not permitted on the patio. See section 10.

## 6. Bike storage room

The ground-floor bike room is for bicycles only, on a first-come basis, and is accessed with
the unit key.

- Bicycles are locked to the racks provided.
- One bicycle per resident named on the lease.
- Nothing else is stored in this room — no boxes, furniture, tyres, or equipment.
- Bicycles left unclaimed after a tenancy ends are removed after written notice.

The bike room is the one shared area at this property intended for storage, and only of
bicycles.

## 7. Trash and recycling

The trash enclosure is at the rear, reached from the parking area.

- Household rubbish in the black bins, bagged and closed.
- Recycling in the blue bins, rinsed and loose. No bagged recycling.
- Cardboard broken down flat.
- Bulky items — furniture, mattresses, appliances, electronics — are **not** left in or
  beside the enclosure. Contact the office to arrange collection.
- Household hazardous waste, including paint, solvents, batteries, and fluorescent tubes,
  is not accepted; the city runs collection points.

## 8. Parking

The property has **four assigned spaces and one tandem space**. Assignment is recorded in
the signed lease. The tandem space is allocated by the office and may be reassigned.

- Park only in the assigned space.
- **There is no visitor parking.** Guests use street parking, subject to city rules.
- **There is no electric vehicle charging**, and vehicles may not be charged from a unit's
  power or a shared outlet.
- Vehicles must be operable and currently registered.
- Vehicle repair beyond adding fluids or changing a tyre is not permitted on the property.
- A vehicle in another unit's space or blocking access may be towed at the owner's expense.

## 9. Guests

A guest may stay **up to 10 days in any 30-day period**. This is a rolling limit, not a
consecutive-day limit: separate visits within the same 30 days count together.

A longer stay requires the office's written consent.

Residents are responsible for their guests' conduct and for making them aware of these
rules, particularly quiet hours, parking, and smoking.

## 10. Smoking and balconies

Smoking is **prohibited** inside every unit and throughout the shared areas — the patio, the
laundry room, the bike room, the trash enclosure, the parking area, and all walkways, stairs
and landings.

**Smoking is permitted on a resident's own private balcony only.**

This covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookah, cannabis in smoked or vaporized form, and
electronic cigarettes and vaporizers.

Residents smoking on a balcony must be mindful of drift into neighbouring units and must
extinguish and dispose of material inside the unit — never over the railing. Balcony
privileges may be withdrawn where drift or fire risk becomes persistent.

## 11. Balconies generally

Balconies are not storage. Permitted: outdoor furniture, plants in stable containers, and a
bicycle where it does not obstruct passage.

Nothing is hung from balcony railings, including laundry, rugs, and towels.

Nothing is drilled into or bolted to the deck, railing, or waterproof membrane. See the
Maintenance & Repair Policy, section 8.

Keep the balcony drain clear where one is fitted.

Satellite dishes, antennas, and air conditioning units require the office's written consent.

## 12. Storage

Beyond the bike room described in section 6, **there is no storage unit, storage locker, or
shared storage area at this property.**

Walkways, stairs, and landings are kept clear at all times.

## 13. Concerns about a neighbour

Residents with a concern about noise or conduct in another unit should contact the office
rather than approach a neighbour directly. Reports may be made in confidence.

**Where a concern involves safety, harassment, or threatening behaviour, contact the office
directly and, where there is immediate danger, emergency services. Concerns of that kind are
handled by staff.**
