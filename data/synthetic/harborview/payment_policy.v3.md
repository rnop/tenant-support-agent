# Payment & Billing Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 July 2024. Superseded 31 March 2025 by version 4.

> Retained for reference. This is not the current policy.

## 1. When rent is due

Rent falls due on the 1st day of each month, in advance and without demand.

## 2. Grace period

Rent received on or before the **5th day of the month** is not treated as late. Rent
received on or after the 6th is late.

> Unchanged in version 4.

## 3. Late fee

The late fee is **5% of the monthly rent**, charged once in any month in which rent is
late.

> Unchanged in version 4.

## 4. Returned payments

- First returned payment during a tenancy: **$25**
- Every subsequent returned payment: **$35**

## 5. How to pay

Rent is paid through the resident portal, or by check or money order delivered to the
office. Cash is not accepted. Autopay is optional.

## 6. Partial payments

Harborview does not accept partial payments of rent. A payment for less than the full
amount due is returned to the resident.

## 7. Administrative fees

- Delivery of a legal notice to the office: **$10**
- Security deposit paid by check: **$5** handling

> Both fees were discontinued on 1 April 2025 and are prohibited under the current policy.
> See version 4, section 7.

## 8. Security deposit limit

The security deposit may not exceed **one month's rent**, effective 1 July 2024.
