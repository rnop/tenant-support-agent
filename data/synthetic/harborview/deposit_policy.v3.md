# Security Deposit Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes version 2.

## 1. The limit

The security deposit collected at Harborview is **capped at one month's rent**.

The cap applies to every unit in the building and does not change according to whether the
unit is let furnished or unfurnished.

## 2. Deposits held by service members

Where a resident is a service member, the one month cap applies without exception.

## 3. The deposit is refundable

The deposit is a refundable sum held against unpaid rent and damage. It is not a fee, not a
cleaning charge, and no part of it is non-refundable.

It is also not rent. A resident may not instruct the office to treat the deposit as the
final month's rent.

## 4. Condition record at move-in

Before the resident takes possession, the office records the condition of the unit and
takes **timestamped photographs** covering each room, each closet, the appliances, and the
fixtures.

A copy of the record and the photographs goes to the resident. This record is what any
later deduction is measured against.

## 5. Requesting an inspection before move-out

A resident may ask for an inspection before the tenancy ends. There is no charge and it is
entirely the resident's choice.

Where the resident requests one, the office gives at least **48 hours' written notice**
before the inspection, unless the resident waives that notice in writing.

Following the inspection the resident receives an itemized list of what would be deducted
if the unit were vacated in its present condition — giving the resident the chance to put
things right first.

## 6. Condition record at move-out

The office photographs the unit once the resident's belongings are out and **before any
cleaning or repair begins**, and photographs it again once that work is finished. All
photographs are timestamped.

## 7. What may be deducted

- rent that remains unpaid
- the cost of cleaning the unit back to the standard of cleanliness recorded at move-in
- repair of damage beyond ordinary wear, caused by the resident, the household, or a guest
- replacing or restoring personal property, where the signed lease so provides

Where work has been carried out, the statement includes the invoice or a good faith
estimate. Where materials were bought, it includes the receipt.

## 8. What may not be deducted

**Ordinary wear and tear is never deducted.** Carpet worn along traffic routes, paint faded
by sunlight, small nail holes, and appliances worn by normal use are ordinary wear.

Deductions are not made for conditions already recorded in the move-in photographs.

## 9. The 21-day statement

The office sends an itemized statement of deductions, together with any balance owing to
the resident, within **21 days** of the tenancy ending and the resident vacating.

The 21 days run from the end of the tenancy. Where the resident returns keys on a different
date, that does not restart the period.

## 10. Method of refund

Where the resident paid rent electronically at any point during the tenancy, the balance is
refunded electronically — unless resident and office have agreed in writing on a different
method.

Where rent was never paid electronically, a check is sent to the forwarding address
supplied by the resident.

Residents should provide a forwarding address before they move out.

## 11. A resident's own deposit

This document sets out the policy that applies at Harborview generally. **It does not
record the deposit held for any unit, the progress of any refund, or the reason for any
deduction.**

**Residents asking about their own deposit are directed to the resident portal or to the
office.**
