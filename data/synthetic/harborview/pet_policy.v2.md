# Pet & Animal Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**

## 1. Assistance animals are not pets

**A service animal or a support animal is not a pet, and nothing in the remainder of this
document applies to one.**

An assistance animal is outside the limits in sections 3 and 4, the restrictions in section
5, and the pet deposit in section 6. **No pet deposit is charged for an assistance animal**,
and no breed, weight, or size restriction is applied to one.

This holds whether the animal is a trained service animal or a support animal connected to a
disability.

**Requests involving an assistance animal go to the office.** They are a reasonable
accommodation matter rather than a pet approval, and they are not decided under this policy
or by automated assistance. A resident who needs an assistance animal, or has questions
about one, should contact the office directly.

The resident remains responsible for damage caused by an assistance animal and for the
animal not becoming a nuisance. That responsibility is separate from the deposit in section
6, which does not apply.

## 2. Approval required

A pet needs the office's written approval before arriving at the property. Approval is
recorded in a Pet Addendum to the lease and covers a named animal; it does not transfer to a
replacement.

## 3. Number of animals

**Maximum two pets per unit.**

## 4. No weight limit

**Harborview applies no weight limit and no breed restriction to pets.**

This differs from Maple Court across the street, which limits pets to 45 pounds. The two
properties have different rules on this point, and a resident should rely on the policy for
the property they live at.

Any animal must still be manageable by its handler in the shared stairwell and entryway,
which are the only routes in and out of the upper units.

## 5. Types of animal

Permitted with approval: dogs, cats, caged birds, fish in tanks of 40 gallons or less, and
small caged animals such as rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, and gerbils.

Not permitted: reptiles, amphibians, arachnids, ferrets, farm animals of any kind, and any
animal prohibited by Long Beach municipal code or California law.

Tanks over 40 gallons need separate written consent because of floor loading in the upper
units.

## 6. Pet deposit — and no pet rent

- **Pet deposit: $500**, refundable, held in addition to the security deposit but within the
  one month total limit on refundable deposits at this property.
- **Pet rent: none.** Harborview charges no monthly pet rent.

This is a second difference from Maple Court, which charges $50 per month in pet rent. A
resident comparing the two properties should not assume the charges are the same.

The pet deposit is refundable except to the extent applied to pet-related damage under
section 10. It is not a non-refundable pet fee.

**Neither the deposit nor any other pet charge applies to an assistance animal.** See
section 1.

## 7. Conduct and control

Dogs are leashed at all times outside the unit — in the stairwell, on the patio, in the
parking area, and on walkways. Cats do not roam the property.

Animals are not tethered anywhere on the property.

Persistent barking, howling, or noise disturbing another unit is a nuisance and is handled
under the Building Rules.

Animals are not permitted in the laundry room or the bike room.

## 8. Balconies

Animals are not left unattended on a balcony at any time.

Animals must not be able to reach through, over, or under a balcony railing. Where a
resident wishes to fit a barrier, that is an alteration and requires the office's written
consent; nothing may be drilled or bolted to the railing or deck.

A balcony is not a substitute for walking a dog, and is not to be used as a toileting area.

## 9. Waste

Animal waste is picked up immediately, bagged, and placed in the trash enclosure — on the
patio, in the parking area, on walkways, and on the adjacent pavement.

Litter is not flushed. Litter and waste bags go in the black bins, never the recycling.

## 10. Damage

Residents are responsible for damage caused by an animal, including scratched doors and
frames, damaged flooring, chewed fixtures, soiled carpet, damage to the balcony surface, and
odour requiring treatment.

Flea or pest treatment made necessary by an animal is charged to the resident.

## 11. Visiting animals

An animal brought by a guest is subject to this policy for the visit, including the leash,
balcony, and waste provisions. A single day's visit needs no approval or fee; anything
longer is treated as a pet under section 2.
