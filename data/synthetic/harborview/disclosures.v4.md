# Required Disclosures Packet — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Built 2004. Four units. Managed by Alder Grove Property Management.

## 1. About this packet

This packet contains the disclosures given to every resident at Harborview when a lease is
signed. It describes conditions and policies at this property. It contains no information
about any individual resident or unit.

Harborview and Maple Court, across the street at 3140 Roswell Avenue, are separate
properties with separate disclosures. Two disclosures required at Maple Court are not
required here — see sections 9 and 10.

## 2. Bed bugs

Bed bugs are small wingless insects that feed on blood, usually at night. Signs of an
infestation include itchy welts, small dark spots on bedding or furniture, shed skins, and
live insects in mattress seams, bed frames, and furniture joints.

Residents should report suspected bed bugs to the office promptly. Early reporting makes
treatment easier and less disruptive.

The office does not show, rent, or re-rent a unit known to be currently infested.

A resident whose unit is inspected is told the results within two business days. Where
shared areas are inspected, every resident is told the results within two business days.

Treatment normally requires the resident to prepare the unit and allow access.

## 3. Mold

The owner is not aware of any mold in the units or shared areas at this property exceeding
levels known to affect health, other than mold visible on ordinary inspection.

Mold follows moisture. Residents can reduce the risk by running the bathroom and kitchen
fans, ventilating the unit, wiping down surfaces where condensation collects, and reporting
leaks and water intrusion promptly.

Reports of water intrusion are treated as urgent maintenance.

## 4. Megan's Law notice

Notice: Pursuant to Section 290.46 of the Penal Code, information about specified
registered sex offenders is made available to the public via an Internet Web site
maintained by the Department of Justice at www.meganslaw.ca.gov. Depending on an offender's
criminal history, this information will include either the address at which the offender
resides or the community of residence and ZIP Code in which the offender resides.

Neither the owner nor Alder Grove Property Management is obliged to consult this database
and neither has done so on any resident's behalf.

## 5. Methamphetamine and fentanyl contamination

The owner has received no order from a health official identifying this property, or any
unit in it, as contaminated by methamphetamine or fentanyl, and is unaware of any such
contamination.

## 6. Smoking policy

Harborview is **not** a wholly smoke-free property. The rule is as follows.

**Smoking is prohibited:**

- inside every unit
- on the rear patio
- in the laundry room
- in the bike storage room
- in and around the trash enclosure
- in the parking area, including inside a parked vehicle
- on walkways, stairs, landings, and entryways

**Smoking is permitted on a resident's own private balcony only.**

The prohibition covers cigarettes, cigars, pipes, hookah, cannabis in smoked or vaporized
form, and electronic cigarettes and vaporizers. The balcony permission covers the same
categories.

Residents smoking on a balcony are asked to be mindful of drift into neighbouring units and
to dispose of material safely. Balcony privileges may be withdrawn where drift or fire risk
becomes persistent.

> This differs from Maple Court, which is smoke-free throughout with no balcony exception.

## 7. Water submetering and utility billing

Harborview was constructed in 2004 and **each unit has its own water submeter**.

**Water** is billed on the unit's own measured consumption. Submeters are read monthly. The
resident statement shows the opening reading, the closing reading, the volume used, and the
rate applied.

**Sewer and trash** are not metered per unit and are billed as a **flat monthly charge per
unit**, identical for all four units.

The rate applied to water is the rate the property is charged by the provider. Nothing is
added — the owner takes no margin, service charge, or administrative fee on utility
billing.

A resident may request their submeter readings and a copy of the provider's bill for any
period in which they were charged. Requests go to the office and are provided at no charge.

Utility charges appear as separate lines on the resident statement and are billed one month
in arrears. They are not rent.

**Electricity and gas** are separately metered and held in the resident's own name with the
provider. They are not billed through the office.

## 8. Rent cap and just cause coverage

**This property is subject to** the rent limits of California Civil Code section 1947.12
and the just cause requirements of California Civil Code section 1946.2, and to Long Beach
Municipal Code chapter 8.99.

Harborview was constructed in 2004. It is not within the exemption for housing issued a
certificate of occupancy within the previous 15 years.

The applicable annual rent increase limit and the required notice are in the Rent Increase
& Renewal Policy.

**Questions about termination of a tenancy, about a notice a resident has received, or
about relocation assistance are handled by staff.**

## 9. Lead-based paint — not applicable

Harborview was constructed in **2004**. The federal lead-based paint disclosure
requirement applies to housing built before 1978 and **does not apply to this property**.
No lead-based paint disclosure is issued for Harborview.

## 10. Asbestos — not applicable

Given the year of construction, the asbestos disclosure issued at Maple Court **does not
apply here**. Residents at Harborview are under no restriction regarding floor tile,
ceiling texture, or pipe insulation on that account.

## 11. Flood hazard

The owner is not aware that this property lies within a special flood hazard area or an
area of potential flooding as designated by the applicable authorities.
