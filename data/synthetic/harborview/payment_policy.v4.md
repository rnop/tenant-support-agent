# Payment & Billing Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 April 2025. Supersedes version 3.

## 1. When rent is due

Rent falls due on the **1st day of each month**, in advance and without demand.

Payment is credited on the day it is received rather than the day it is sent. A payment
arriving after the office closes is treated as received on the next business day.

## 2. Grace period

**Harborview allows a grace period of five days.**

Rent received on or before the **5th day of the month** is not treated as late. Rent
received on or after the **6th** is late.

Where the 5th falls on a weekend or a public holiday, the grace period runs to the end of
the next business day.

A grace period is a term of this property's lease. California law does not require one, and
it is not a feature of every property — the grace period here differs from the one at Maple
Court across the street.

## 3. Late fee

The late fee at Harborview is **5% of the monthly rent**.

It is charged once in any month in which rent is late. It does not accrue daily and it does
not compound.

Because the fee is a percentage, it varies with the rent for the unit. The Payment & Billing
Policy does not state the rent for any unit, so it does not state the resulting fee amount
for any unit.

California fixes no maximum late fee by statute. The percentage here is set by the lease as
a reasonable estimate of the administrative cost of processing a late payment.

## 4. Returned payments

Where a payment is returned unpaid by the resident's bank, it is treated as never having
been made.

- First returned payment during a tenancy: **$25**
- Every subsequent returned payment: **$35**

A returned payment does not extend the due date or the grace period. Where the replacement
payment arrives on or after the 6th, the late fee in section 3 applies as well.

Following a second returned payment the office may require future rent by a method that
does not depend on a bank clearing the payment.

## 5. How to pay

Rent is paid through the resident portal by bank transfer or card, or by check or money
order delivered to the office. Cash is not accepted.

Autopay is offered through the portal and is optional. Residents using autopay remain
responsible for confirming the payment was drawn.

## 6. Partial payments are not accepted

**Harborview does not accept partial payments of rent.** A payment for less than the full
amount due is returned to the resident, and the rent remains unpaid.

This differs from the practice at Maple Court, which does accept partial payments. The two
properties have different policies on this point.

A resident who cannot pay the full amount by the 5th should contact the office **before**
the due date to discuss arrangements. An arrangement agreed in writing with the office is
not a partial payment for the purposes of this section.

## 7. Fees that are not charged

Harborview does not charge:

- a fee for serving or delivering a legal notice to the owner or the office
- a fee for paying the security deposit by check
- a fee for submitting a maintenance request
- a fee for an on-time rent payment by any accepted method

## 8. Utility charges

Water is submetered per unit and billed on measured consumption. Sewer and trash are billed
as a flat monthly charge per unit. Both appear as separate lines on the resident statement.

Electricity and gas are held in the resident's own name and are not billed through the
office.

Details of the submeter reading cycle and the resident's right to see readings and bills are
in the Required Disclosures Packet and the Utility Allocation Addendum.

Utility charges are not rent. A dispute about a utility charge does not excuse payment of
rent.

## 9. Notices about unpaid rent

Where rent remains unpaid the office may serve a written notice. A notice to pay rent allows
three days, not counting Saturdays, Sundays, or judicial holidays.

**A resident who has received a notice should contact the office. Questions about a notice,
about what follows it, or about a resident's own balance are handled by staff and are not
answered from this document.**
