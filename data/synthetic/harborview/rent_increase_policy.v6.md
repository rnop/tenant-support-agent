# Rent Increase & Renewal Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 August 2026. Supersedes version 5.

## 1. Coverage

Harborview is subject to the rent limits in California Civil Code section 1947.12.

The building was completed in 2004 and falls outside the exemption for housing issued a
certificate of occupancy within the previous 15 years.

## 2. The limit for the current cap year

The cap year runs from **1 August to 31 July**.

For the cap year that began **1 August 2026**, the maximum increase over any twelve-month
period at this property is **8.7%**.

The figure for the cap year that ended 31 July 2026 was **8.0%**. It is retained in version
5 of this policy. An increase served under the earlier cap year is governed by the figure in
force when notice was given, not by the figure in this version.

## 3. How the limit is calculated

The statutory formula is 5% plus the year-over-year change in the regional Consumer Price
Index for the area in which the property sits, subject to an absolute ceiling of 10%.

Long Beach falls in the Los Angeles County region for this purpose.

The limit is a ceiling. It is not a scheduled or automatic increase, and it does not mean
rent rises in any given year.

## 4. Frequency

Rent may be raised no more than **twice in any twelve-month period**, and the increases
taken together must stay within the limit in section 2.

## 5. Notice

No increase takes effect until written notice has been given.

- **30 days'** notice where the increase, combined with any other increase in the preceding
  twelve months, is 10% or less.
- **90 days'** notice where the combined increase exceeds 10%.

Since the ceiling at this property is under 10%, increases here ordinarily require 30 days'
notice.

The notice states the new amount and the date from which it applies. A copy is posted to the
resident portal.

## 6. Renewal

The office contacts residents about renewal ordinarily 60 to 90 days before a fixed term
ends.

Where no new fixed term is signed, the tenancy continues month to month on the existing
terms, subject to any change made by notice under section 5.

## 7. A resident's own rent

This document states the limits and notice rules for the property. **It does not state the
rent for any unit, whether a unit's rent is being increased, by how much, or from when.**

Residents asking about an increase to their own rent are directed to the notice itself, to
the resident portal, or to the office.
