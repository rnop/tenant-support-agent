# Standard Residential Lease Terms — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Managed by Alder Grove Property Management

## 1. Scope of these terms

This document sets out the standard lease terms offered at Harborview Apartments. It is a
template. It is not a signed agreement and it records no particular tenancy.

An executed lease may differ from this document. Where a signed lease or an attached
addendum provides otherwise, the signed document controls. A resident who wants to know
what their own lease says should consult their signed copy or contact the office.

Harborview is a separate property from Maple Court at 3140 Roswell Avenue, across the
street. The two have different terms. This document applies to Harborview only.

## 2. Premises and occupancy

The premises are a single residential unit at Harborview Apartments, identified by unit
number in the signed lease. The building contains four units.

The unit is for residential use only. Occupancy is limited to the persons named in the
signed lease. Adding an occupant requires the owner's written consent.

## 3. Term

The initial term is stated in the signed lease and is ordinarily twelve months. Unless the
signed lease provides otherwise, the tenancy continues month to month once the initial term
expires.

Term length, start date, and end date are recorded in the signed lease:
`[            ]`.

## 4. Rent

Rent is due on the **1st day of each month**, payable in advance without demand.

The monthly rent amount is stated in the signed lease: `[            ]`.

A payment is credited on the date it is received, not the date it is sent.

## 5. Grace period and late payment

**Harborview allows a grace period of five days.** Rent received on or before the **5th day
of the month** is not treated as late. A late fee applies from the 6th.

The late fee is **5% of the monthly rent**, charged once in any month in which rent is
late. It does not accrue daily.

California sets no statutory late fee amount and requires no grace period. Both figures
above are terms of this lease, and they are specific to this property.

## 6. Security deposit

The security deposit may not exceed **one month's rent**. The amount is recorded in the
signed lease: `[            ]`.

The deposit is not rent and may not be applied by the resident as the final month's rent.

Handling, itemization, and return are covered by the Security Deposit Policy. Deposits are
itemized and returned within 21 days after the tenancy ends.

## 7. Utilities

Water is **submetered by unit**. Each unit has its own water submeter and is billed for its
own measured consumption. Sewer and trash are billed as a flat monthly charge per unit.

Details are in the Required Disclosures Packet and the Utility Allocation Addendum.

Electricity and gas are separately metered and held in the resident's name.

## 8. Condition of the premises

The unit is let in the condition existing at move-in, as recorded in the move-in inspection
and accompanying photographs.

The owner maintains the premises in a habitable condition as required by California law.

## 9. Maintenance and repairs

Residents report maintenance through the channels in the Maintenance & Repair Policy, which
sets out priorities and target response times.

Residents are responsible for damage beyond ordinary wear caused by the resident, a member
of the household, or a guest.

Residents replace smoke and carbon monoxide detector batteries during the tenancy and
report any detector that does not function.

## 10. Owner access to the unit

The owner or the owner's agent may enter the unit for purposes permitted by California
Civil Code section 1954.

Except in an emergency, entry requires **at least 24 hours' written notice** stating the
date, approximate time, and purpose, and occurs during normal business hours.

No advance notice is required in an emergency.

## 11. Alterations

Painting, fixture changes, and any alteration require the owner's written consent. Wall
anchors, mounted shelving, and mounted televisions are alterations for this purpose.

## 12. Use of shared areas

Harborview has a shared laundry room, a rear patio, a bike storage room, and a trash
enclosure. Hours and conditions of use are in the Building Rules & Shared Areas document.

Shared areas are not storage, with the exception of the bike room for its intended purpose.

## 13. Quiet enjoyment

Residents are entitled to quiet enjoyment of the unit, and are responsible for their own
conduct and their guests' conduct not interfering with the quiet enjoyment of the other
three units.

Quiet hours are **11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.**

## 14. Animals

Pets are permitted subject to the Pet & Animal Policy, which sets the limits on number and
type and states the pet deposit.

**Assistance animals are not pets.** Service animals and support animals are not subject to
the pet limits or the pet deposit in this lease or in the Pet & Animal Policy. Requests
involving an assistance animal are handled by the office and are not decided under the pet
provisions.

## 15. Smoking

Smoking is prohibited inside every unit and in all shared areas.

**Smoking is permitted on a resident's own private balcony only.** It is not permitted on
the patio, in the laundry room, in the bike room, in the trash enclosure, in the parking
area, or on walkways, stairs, or landings.

Residents smoking on a balcony are asked to be mindful of drift into neighbouring units and
to dispose of material safely. Balcony privileges may be withdrawn where drift or fire risk
becomes a persistent problem.

## 16. Parking

The building has four assigned parking spaces and one tandem space. Assignment is recorded
in the signed lease. There is no visitor parking and no electric vehicle charging.

Parking rules are in the Building Rules & Shared Areas document.

## 17. Renters insurance

Residents maintain renters insurance with personal liability coverage of at least
**$300,000**, and provide proof at move-in and on renewal.

## 18. Guests

A guest may stay up to **10 days in any 30-day period**. A longer stay requires the
owner's written consent and may require the guest to be added to the lease.

Note that this is a rolling limit rather than a consecutive-day limit.

## 19. Ending the tenancy

A resident ending a month-to-month tenancy gives **30 days' written notice**.

An owner ending a month-to-month tenancy gives 30 days' written notice where the resident
has occupied the unit less than one year, and 60 days' written notice at one year or more.

This property is subject to the just cause requirements of California Civil Code section
1946.2 and to Long Beach Municipal Code chapter 8.99. **Questions about ending a tenancy,
about a notice a resident has received, or about relocation assistance are directed to
staff and are not answered from this document.**

## 20. Rent increases and renewal

This property is subject to the rent limits of California Civil Code section 1947.12.

The applicable annual limit, the required notice, and the renewal process are in the Rent
Increase & Renewal Policy. The amount of any increase for a particular unit appears in the
notice sent to that unit and in the portal; it is not stated here.

## 21. Addenda and disclosures

The signed lease may include addenda that change or add to these terms. Addenda used at
Harborview include the Pet Addendum, the Smoke-Free Addendum, the Mold & Moisture Addendum,
the Bed Bug Addendum, and the Utility Allocation Addendum.

Required disclosures are in the Required Disclosures Packet.

**Harborview was constructed in 2004. The federal lead-based paint disclosure does not
apply to this property**, which is a difference from Maple Court across the street.

A resident who wants to know which addenda are attached to their own lease should consult
their signed copy or contact the office.
