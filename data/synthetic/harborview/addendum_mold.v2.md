## 1. What this addendum is

Indoor mold follows moisture. Almost every indoor mold problem starts with a water source —
a leak, condensation, or humidity that is not ventilated away. Control the moisture and the
mold does not establish.

This addendum sets out what each party does about moisture. It is attached to every lease at
Harborview.

## 2. The owner's statement

The owner is **not aware of any mold in this unit or in the shared areas** exceeding levels
known to affect health, other than mold visible on ordinary inspection.

The unit was inspected before the tenancy and any moisture damage found was repaired.

## 3. Balconies and water intrusion

Harborview has a private balcony to each unit. **The most common source of water intrusion at
this property is a blocked balcony drain**, which backs water up under the door threshold and
into the unit, or into the unit below.

Keeping the balcony drain clear is a resident responsibility under section 4 and a reporting
obligation under section 5. A blocked drain is treated as urgent maintenance.

Nothing may be drilled into or bolted to the balcony deck or its waterproof membrane. A
penetration through the membrane admits water into the structure and is not always visible
from above.

## 4. What the resident agrees to do

- Run the bathroom fan during and for at least 20 minutes after showering or bathing.
- Run the kitchen fan when cooking, especially when boiling or steaming.
- Ventilate the unit. Open windows and the balcony door when weather allows.
- Wipe down surfaces where condensation collects — window frames, sills, bathroom tile and
  glass.
- Leave a gap between furniture and exterior walls, and do not pack closets tight against
  them.
- **Keep the balcony drain clear of leaves, soil, and debris.**
- Dry spills, overflows, and tracked-in water promptly.
- Hang wet towels and clothing to dry rather than leaving them piled.
- Keep heating at a reasonable level in cold weather.
- Not block or disable any ventilation fan or vent.

## 5. What the resident agrees to report

Promptly, to the office:

- any leak, drip, or water intrusion, however small
- any overflow from a bath, shower, sink, or toilet
- **any standing water on the balcony, or a balcony drain that is slow or blocked**
- any damp patch, staining, bubbling paint, or swollen woodwork
- any visible mold growth
- a persistent musty or earthy smell
- a ventilation fan that does not work
- a window, door, or balcony threshold that admits rain

**Water intrusion is urgent maintenance.** Early reporting is the single most effective step
a resident can take.

## 6. What the owner does

On receiving a report the owner inspects, identifies and repairs the moisture source, and
remediates affected material.

Remediation beyond a small surface area is carried out by contractors experienced in mold
remediation.

The owner inspects balcony waterproofing and drainage on a schedule as part of the
maintenance programme.

## 7. Access

Inspection and remediation require access. Other than in an emergency the office gives at
least 24 hours' written notice. Substantial water intrusion is an emergency and does not
require advance notice.
