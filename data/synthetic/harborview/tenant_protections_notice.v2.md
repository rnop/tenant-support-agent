# Just Cause & Relocation Notice — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**

## 1. Read this first

**This notice is background information. It does not decide anything about any particular
tenancy.**

A resident who has received a notice, been asked to leave, or has a question about their
own tenancy ending should speak to the office directly and, if they wish, to independent
legal assistance. These matters are handled by staff. They are not addressed by automated
assistance and they are not resolved from this document.

Nothing here states whether a given notice is valid, whether a given reason qualifies, or
what any particular resident is owed.

## 2. When the protections apply

Harborview is covered by the just cause requirements of California Civil Code section
1946.2 and by Long Beach Municipal Code chapter 8.99.

Under both, protection begins once a resident has lawfully occupied the unit for **twelve
continuous months**. Where an additional adult joined the tenancy later, the timing can work
differently.

Where Long Beach's ordinance is stricter than the state statute, the Long Beach requirement
applies.

## 3. Reasons recognised by the ordinance

Where the protections apply, a tenancy ends only for a reason the ordinance and the statute
recognise. These divide into two kinds.

**Reasons involving the tenancy itself** — non-payment of rent, breach of a material lease
term, nuisance, and similar. Where a breach can be put right, the resident is first given a
chance to do so.

**Reasons not involving the resident's conduct** — the owner or a qualifying family member
moving in, removal of the property from the rental market, substantial remodel, or
compliance with a government order.

Relocation assistance is owed for the second kind. It is not owed for the first.

## 4. Relocation assistance

Where a tenancy at Harborview ends for a no-fault reason, the resident is entitled to
relocation assistance equal to **one month's rent**.

It is provided either as a payment made before or at the time the notice to vacate is
served, or as a written waiver of the final month's rent. Which method is used is the
owner's choice.

Long Beach requirements can go beyond the state minimum in particular circumstances,
including substantial remodel.

**What is owed in any individual case turns on the facts of that case, and this section
does not determine it.**

## 5. Where to get help

- Alder Grove Property Management — the office, during business hours
- City of Long Beach, Housing & Neighborhood Services, tenant and landlord resources
- Legal aid organisations serving Long Beach, which advise qualifying residents at no cost

A resident who has received any written notice should keep it, note the date it arrived, and
take it with them to whichever of these they approach.
