# Maintenance & Repair Policy — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**

## 1. How to make a request

Non-emergency requests go through the **resident portal**. This is the preferred route
because it timestamps the request and keeps a record.

Requests may also be made by telephone or in person at the office during business hours, or
in writing.

Describe the problem, the location, and when it began. Requests are acknowledged and the
resident is told what priority was assigned.

## 2. Priority levels

**Emergency.** Any condition listed in section 3. Attended immediately, at any hour.

**Urgent.** A condition materially affecting habitability or security without being an
immediate danger — a failed refrigerator, the only toilet in the unit out of service, a
contained but persistent leak, a failed interior lock, a failed balcony door latch. Target
first response within **24 hours**.

**Routine.** Everything else — dripping taps, running toilets, faulty light fittings,
sticking windows, worn seals, cosmetic damage. Target first response within **five business
days**.

Target times are for a first response, which may be an inspection rather than a completed
repair. Parts and contractor availability affect completion.

## 3. What counts as an emergency

Treated as emergencies at this property:

- gas odour or a suspected gas leak
- smoke or fire
- flooding, or an uncontrolled water leak
- no heat
- carbon monoxide detector activation, or suspected carbon monoxide
- no water, or total loss of water service to the unit
- an electrical hazard — sparking, burning smell, exposed live wiring, failed panel
- a broken exterior door lock, broken exterior window, or any condition leaving the unit
  unsecured
- a sewage backup into the unit
- a failure of the balcony structure or railing
- any condition making the unit unsafe to occupy

**Emergencies are reported by telephone, not through the portal.** See section 4.

## 4. Reporting an emergency

Emergencies go to the **24-hour maintenance line**, answered at all hours including
weekends and public holidays. The number is on the Property Fact Sheet, in the portal, and
posted in the laundry room.

Where anyone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services first and the maintenance
line afterwards.

The maintenance line exists to take the report. What a resident should do in the moment
depends on the situation and is directed by emergency services or by the person answering
the line.

## 5. Access to the unit

Repairs need access. Other than in an emergency, the office gives at least **24 hours'
written notice**, stating date, approximate time, and purpose, and attends during normal
business hours.

**No advance notice is required in an emergency.**

A resident may agree to shorter notice or a fixed appointment. A resident wishing to be
present should say so when making the request.

Where a pet is kept, the office asks that it be secured during the visit.

## 6. Owner responsibilities

The owner keeps the unit habitable, including:

- weatherproofing of roof and exterior walls
- plumbing, gas, and electrical systems in working order
- hot and cold running water and a working sewage connection
- heating in working order
- floors, stairs, and railings in good repair
- **balcony structures, railings, and waterproofing**, inspected on a schedule
- working smoke and carbon monoxide detectors at the start of the tenancy
- building, grounds, and shared areas clean and free of pests

The owner maintains the patio, the landscaping, the building exterior, the laundry
equipment, and the bike room.

## 7. Resident responsibilities

Residents are responsible for:

- keeping the unit clean and sanitary
- disposing of rubbish properly using the trash enclosure
- using plumbing, electrical, and gas fixtures properly
- **replacing smoke and carbon monoxide detector batteries during the tenancy**, and
  reporting any detector that does not work
- replacing light bulbs in unit fixtures
- reporting leaks, damp, visible mold, and pest activity promptly

## 8. Balcony and patio maintenance

Residents keep their balcony clear of accumulated debris and keep the drain clear where one
is fitted. A blocked balcony drain causes water intrusion into the unit below and is
reported as urgent.

Residents must not drill into, bolt to, or otherwise penetrate the balcony deck, railing, or
waterproof membrane. This includes fixing screens, privacy panels, planters, and lighting.
Requests of that kind go to the office.

Where smoking on a balcony is permitted under the lease, material must be fully extinguished
and disposed of in the unit, never over the railing.

## 9. Damage beyond ordinary wear

Repairs needed because of damage caused by the resident, the household, or a guest, beyond
ordinary wear, are charged to the resident.

Ordinary wear is not charged. Carpet worn in traffic routes, faded paint, and small nail
holes are ordinary wear.

Where a charge applies the resident is told before work proceeds, other than in an emergency
that cannot wait.

## 10. Status of a specific request

This document explains how maintenance works here. **It holds no information about the
status of any request, the schedule for any visit, or the history of any unit.**

Residents asking about a request they have made are directed to the resident portal, which
shows open requests, or to the office.
