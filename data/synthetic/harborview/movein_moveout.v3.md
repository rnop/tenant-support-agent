# Move-In & Move-Out Procedures — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 January 2026. Supersedes version 2.

## 1. Before keys are released

The office requires the signed lease and addenda, the security deposit and first month's
rent in cleared funds, and proof of renters insurance meeting the **$300,000** liability
minimum for this property.

## 2. Move-in inspection and photographs

The office walks the unit with the resident before possession, recording the condition of
each room, the appliances, the fixtures, the flooring, the paint, and **the balcony**.

**Timestamped photographs** are taken at the same time, covering each room, each closet, the
appliances, the fixtures, and the balcony surface and railing.

The resident receives the inspection record and the photographs. A resident who disagrees
with any part of the record should say so in writing within **five days** of move-in; the
record is then amended or the disagreement noted on it.

This record is what any end-of-tenancy deduction is measured against.

## 3. Keys and laundry card

Issued at the move-in appointment: unit key, mailbox key, and a laundry card for the
card-operated machines. The unit key also opens the bike room.

Additional and replacement keys and cards come from the office. **Residents may not rekey or
fit an additional lock without the office's written consent.**

Laundry cards are topped up through the resident portal.

## 4. Utilities at move-in

Electricity and gas are held in the resident's name and should be transferred to take effect
on the lease start date.

Water is submetered. The office records the submeter reading at move-in and it appears on
the inspection record. Sewer and trash are billed at a flat monthly rate per unit. None of
these require action by the resident.

## 5. Move windows

Moving in or out is permitted **9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday**. No moving on
Sundays or public holidays.

This is narrower than the window at Maple Court across the street, because the stairwell at
Harborview is shared by all four units and is the only route to the upper floor.

Walkways, stairs, landings, and the patio must not be obstructed. Packing material is broken
down and removed by the resident, not left at the trash enclosure.

A moving vehicle may use the parking area temporarily but must not block another unit's
space. Tell the office in advance where a large vehicle is expected.

## 6. Giving notice to move out

A resident ending a month-to-month tenancy gives **30 days' written notice**, delivered to
the office or submitted through the portal.

Notice given mid-month runs 30 days from receipt, not to the end of the calendar month,
unless the signed lease says otherwise.

A resident leaving at the end of a fixed term should confirm in writing.

## 7. The optional pre-move-out inspection

A resident may request an inspection before the tenancy ends. It is optional and free.

The office gives at least **48 hours' written notice** unless the resident waives it in
writing. It ordinarily takes place in the final two weeks, and the resident may attend.

Afterwards the resident receives an itemized statement of what would presently be deducted,
allowing time to put those items right.

## 8. Cleaning expected at move-out

The unit should be left as clean as it was at move-in, as shown in the move-in photographs.

That ordinarily means appliances emptied and cleaned inside and out, cabinets and drawers
emptied and wiped, bathroom fixtures cleaned, floors swept and mopped or vacuumed, windows
and sills wiped, light fittings dusted, **the balcony swept and its drain clear**, and all
personal property and rubbish removed.

Residents are not expected to repaint, shampoo merely worn carpet, or repair ordinary wear.

## 9. Move-out photographs

The office photographs the unit once belongings are removed and **before any cleaning or
repair begins**, and again after that work is finished. All photographs are timestamped and
support any deduction.

## 10. Returning keys and card

All keys and the laundry card are returned to the office by the end of the last day of the
tenancy. Rent continues to accrue while keys are held.

## 11. Deposit accounting

Within **21 days** of the tenancy ending the office sends an itemized statement of
deductions with any balance owing, together with invoices, estimates, or receipts.

Where the resident paid rent electronically, the refund is sent electronically unless the
parties have agreed in writing on another method. Otherwise a check is sent.

## 12. Forwarding address

Give the office a forwarding address before moving out. Where none is provided, the
statement and any refund go to the last known address, which is the unit being vacated.
