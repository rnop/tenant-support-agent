# Property Fact Sheet — Harborview Apartments

## 1. The property

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **Harborview Apartments** |
| Address | **3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814** |
| Units | 4 |
| Built | 2004 |
| Type | Two-storey apartment building with private balconies |

Harborview is on the **east side of Roswell Avenue**. Maple Court, also managed by Alder
Grove, is directly across the street at 3140 Roswell Avenue. **The two are separate
properties with separate rules, separate policies, and separate documents.** See section 7.

## 2. Management office

Alder Grove Property Management

- **Office hours:** Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Closed weekends and public
  holidays.
- **Telephone:** (562) 555-0142
- **Email:** office@aldergrovepm.example

The office serves both Roswell Avenue properties from the same number and address. **When
contacting the office, say which property you are calling about** — the contact details do
not distinguish them.

## 3. Emergency maintenance

**24-hour maintenance line: (562) 555-0199**

Answered at all hours, including nights, weekends, and public holidays. Use it for gas
odour, smoke or fire, flooding, no heat, carbon monoxide, no water, an electrical hazard, a
sewage backup, a balcony structural failure, or a unit left unsecured.

**Where anyone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services on 911 first.**

This is the same line that serves Maple Court. Say which property and unit you are calling
from.

Non-emergency requests go through the resident portal, not this line.

## 4. Resident portal

**portal.aldergrovepm.example**

Residents use the portal to pay rent, set up autopay, top up the laundry card, submit and
track maintenance requests, view their own statements and balance, view notices, and upload
proof of renters insurance.

**Anything specific to a resident's own tenancy — balance, payment history, request status,
lease documents, notices, submeter readings — is in the portal.** The office can help with
access.

## 5. On-site

- Shared laundry room, ground floor, card-operated
- Rear patio with a shared gas grill
- Bike storage room, ground floor, accessed with the unit key
- Trash and recycling enclosure at the rear
- Four assigned parking spaces plus one tandem space
- Private balconies to each unit
- Mailboxes at the front entrance

## 6. What is not at this property

Harborview has **no** pool, gym, clubhouse, package room or package acceptance, storage
units or lockers beyond the bike room, visitor parking, electric vehicle charging, or
on-site staff.

## 7. How this property differs from Maple Court

The two buildings face each other and share a manager, so residents sometimes assume the
terms match. Several do not:

| | Harborview | Maple Court |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grace period | 5 days | None |
| Late fee | 5% of monthly rent | $75 flat |
| Partial payments | Not accepted | Accepted |
| Quiet hours | 11:00 p.m. – 7:00 a.m. | 10:00 p.m. – 8:00 a.m. |
| Smoking | Private balcony only | Smoke-free throughout |
| Pet weight limit | None | 45 lb |
| Pet rent | None | $50 per month |
| Guests | 10 days in any 30 | 14 consecutive days |
| Renters insurance | $300,000 | $100,000 |
| Laundry | Card-operated | Coin-operated |
| Water billing | Submetered per unit | Allocated in equal quarters |
| Lead paint disclosure | Not applicable (2004) | Required (1962) |

**A resident should rely on the documents for the property they live at.**
