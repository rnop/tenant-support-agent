# Building Rules & Shared Areas — Harborview Apartments

**Harborview Apartments, 3145 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814**
Effective 1 June 2022. Superseded 28 February 2025 by version 3.

> Retained for reference. This is not the current set of rules. Quiet hours changed in
> version 3, and version 3 introduced the balcony smoking provision, which does not appear
> here.

## 1. Scope

These rules bind residents, members of the household, and guests.

## 2. Quiet hours

Quiet hours run from **10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.**, every day.

> Changed in version 3 to 11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.

## 3. Laundry room

Card-operated washers and dryers shared by the four units, on the ground floor. Open 6:00
a.m. to 11:00 p.m., first-come basis. Remove laundry promptly. Clean the lint filter after
use.

## 4. Rear patio

Shared by the four units, available 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Charcoal and open-flame grills
are not permitted. A shared gas grill is provided.

## 5. Bike storage room

For bicycles only, accessed with the unit key. One bicycle per resident named on the lease.
Nothing else is stored in this room.

## 6. Trash

Household rubbish in the black bins, bagged. Recycling in the blue bins, rinsed and loose.
Cardboard broken down. Bulky items arranged through the office.

## 7. Parking

Four assigned spaces and one tandem space. Park only in the assigned space. There is no
visitor parking. Vehicles must be operable and registered.

## 8. Guests

A guest may stay up to 10 days in any 30-day period. A longer stay requires the office's
written consent.

## 9. Smoking

Smoking is prohibited inside every unit and in all shared areas, including the patio, the
laundry room, the bike room, the trash enclosure, the parking area, and all walkways.

> Version 3 added an exception permitting smoking on a resident's own private balcony. That
> exception does not form part of this version.
