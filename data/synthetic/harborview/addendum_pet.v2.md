# Pet Addendum (Template) — Harborview Apartments

**This is a blank template. It is not a signed agreement and it records no actual tenancy.**

## 1. What this addendum is

Attached to a signed lease at Harborview where the resident has been approved to keep a pet.
It records the animal and the terms.

**Where attached to a lease, this addendum's terms apply in addition to the Pet & Animal
Policy, and where the two differ, this addendum governs for that tenancy.** A resident
should therefore check their own signed lease rather than rely on the general policy.

## 2. Not for assistance animals

**A service animal or a support animal is not a pet and is not recorded on this form.** No
pet deposit is charged for an assistance animal, and no weight or breed restriction applies
to one.

Assistance animals are handled by the office as a reasonable accommodation. Do not use this
form.

The standard pet deposit here is $500, refundable. **Harborview charges no pet rent.**

The deposit is refundable except to the extent applied to pet-related damage. It is not a
non-refundable fee.

## 5. Conditions

The resident agrees that:

- the animal is leashed at all times outside the unit, including in the shared stairwell
- the animal is never tethered on the property
- waste is picked up immediately and placed in the trash enclosure
- the animal is not taken into the laundry room or the bike room
- persistent noise disturbing another unit will be addressed
- the resident is responsible for all damage caused by the animal
- vaccinations and any required licence are kept current
- the animal is manageable by its handler in the shared stairwell and entryway

## 6. Balcony conditions

- The animal is **never left unattended on the balcony**.
- The animal must not be able to reach through, over, or under the railing.
- The balcony is not used as a toileting area.
- Any barrier or screen is an alteration requiring the office's written consent. Nothing is
  drilled or bolted to the railing, deck, or waterproof membrane.

## 7. Withdrawal of approval

Approval may be withdrawn where the animal causes repeated disturbance, damage, or a threat
to others' safety, following written notice and a reasonable opportunity to resolve it.
