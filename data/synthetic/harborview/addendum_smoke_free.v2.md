## 1. What this addendum is

California Civil Code section 1947.5 requires a lease that limits smoking to say so and to
identify the areas where smoking is prohibited. This addendum does that and is attached to
every lease at Harborview.

**Harborview is not a wholly smoke-free property.** It permits smoking in one place only.
This is a difference from Maple Court across the street, which prohibits smoking everywhere.

## 2. Where smoking is prohibited

- inside every unit, including all rooms, closets, and bathrooms
- on the rear patio
- in the laundry room
- in the bike storage room
- in and around the trash enclosure
- in the parking area, including inside a parked vehicle
- on all walkways, stairs, landings, and entryways
- anywhere else on the grounds

## 3. Where smoking is permitted

**On the resident's own private balcony, and nowhere else on the property.**

This permission extends to the resident and to guests while on that balcony. It does not
extend to another unit's balcony.

## 4. What is covered

Smoking means inhaling, exhaling, burning, carrying, or holding any lighted or heated
product intended for inhalation, including cigarettes, cigarillos, cigars, pipes, hookah and
waterpipe, cannabis in any smoked or vaporized form, and electronic cigarettes, vape pens,
and any device producing an inhaled vapour or aerosol.

Both the prohibition in section 2 and the permission in section 3 cover all of these.

## 5. Conditions on balcony smoking

The permission in section 3 is subject to the following, and depends on them being observed:

- Material is fully extinguished and disposed of **inside the unit**. Nothing is dropped or
  flicked over the railing, onto the patio, or into the parking area.
- Ash and butts are not left on the balcony surface.
- The resident takes reasonable care to limit drift into neighbouring units, including by
  not smoking directly beside an open window or door of an adjoining unit.

**Balcony smoking privileges may be withdrawn for a unit** where drift into other units, or
fire risk, becomes a persistent problem, after written notice and an opportunity to resolve
it. Withdrawal makes the whole property smoke-free for that unit.

## 6. Guests

The resident is responsible for informing guests of this policy and for guests observing it
while on the property.

## 7. Breach

Smoking outside the permitted area is a breach of the lease.

The resident is responsible for the cost of remediating smoke odour and residue in the unit,
which can include sealing, repainting, and replacing soft furnishings supplied with the
unit.

## 8. What the owner does not promise

The owner does not promise that the property is or will remain free of smoke drift. Because
smoking is permitted on balconies, **drift between units is foreseeable at this property.**

This addendum states rules and the owner's intention to enforce them. It is not a guarantee
about air quality.

A resident troubled by smoke reaching their unit should report it to the office.
