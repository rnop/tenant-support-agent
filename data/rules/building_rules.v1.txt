Building Rules & Shared Areas — Maple Court
===========================================
Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2023

Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814
Effective 1 January 2023. Superseded 31 May 2025 by version 2.

    This version is retained for reference. It is not the current set of
    rules. Quiet hours and laundry room hours both changed in version 2.

1. Scope
--------

These rules apply to residents, members of the household, and guests.

2. Quiet hours
--------------

Quiet hours are 11:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m., every day.

    Changed in version 2 to 10:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m.

3. Laundry room
---------------

The laundry room contains coin-operated machines shared by the four units.

Open 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Machines are used on a first-come basis. Remove
laundry promptly. Clean the lint filter after each use. Report faults to the
office.

    Hours changed in version 2 to 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

4. Courtyard
------------

The courtyard is shared by the four units and is available from 8:00 a.m. to
10:00 p.m.

Charcoal and open-flame grills are not permitted.

5. Trash
--------

Household rubbish goes in the black bins, bagged. Recycling goes in the blue
bins, rinsed and loose. Break down cardboard. Bulky items are arranged through
the office.

6. Parking
----------

Four spaces, one assigned per unit. Park only in the assigned space. There is
no visitor parking. Vehicles must be operable and registered.

7. Guests
---------

A guest may stay up to 14 consecutive days. A longer stay requires the
office's written consent.

8. Smoking
----------

Maple Court is smoke-free throughout, including inside units, on balconies and
patios, in the courtyard, and in the parking area.
