Security Deposit Policy — Maple Court
=====================================
Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2026

Maple Court, 3140 Roswell Avenue, Long Beach, CA 90814
Effective 1 January 2026.

1. Deposit limit
----------------

The security deposit at Maple Court is not more than one month's rent.

This limit applies to every unit at this property regardless of whether the
unit is furnished.

A resident who is a service member is entitled to the one month limit in all
cases.

2. What the deposit is not
--------------------------

The deposit is not rent. A resident may not direct that the deposit be applied
as the last month's rent.

The deposit is not a cleaning fee and is not a non-refundable charge. Any
amount collected as a security deposit is refundable except to the extent it
is properly deducted under section 6.

3. Photographs at move-in
-------------------------

The office photographs the unit at move-in, before the resident takes
possession. The photographs are timestamped and cover each room, each closet,
the appliances, and the fixtures.

The resident receives a copy of the move-in inspection and the photographs.

4. Initial inspection before move-out
-------------------------------------

A resident may request an inspection before moving out. The office gives at
least 48 hours' written notice of the inspection unless the resident agrees in
writing to waive that notice.

The inspection produces an itemized statement of what the office would
presently deduct, so the resident has an opportunity to remedy those items
before the tenancy ends.

The inspection is optional. A resident who does not request one keeps every
other right under this policy.

5. Photographs at move-out
--------------------------

The office photographs the unit after the resident's possessions are removed
and before any cleaning or repair work begins, and again after that work is
complete. These photographs are timestamped.

6. Permitted deductions
-----------------------

Deductions may be made only for:

  - unpaid rent
  - cleaning the unit to the level of cleanliness it had at the start of the
    tenancy
  - repair of damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, caused by the resident, a
    member of the household, or a guest
  - restoring or replacing personal property, where the signed lease provides
    for it

Ordinary wear and tear is not deductible. Faded paint, worn carpet in traffic
paths, minor nail holes, and appliance wear from normal use are ordinary wear.

7. Itemization and return
-------------------------

Within 21 days after the tenancy ends and the resident has vacated, the office
sends an itemized statement of every deduction together with any remaining
balance of the deposit.

Where a deduction is for work performed, the statement includes the invoice or
a good faith estimate. Where it is for materials, it includes the receipt.

The 21-day period runs from the end of the tenancy, not from the date the
resident returns the keys, if those differ.

8. How the refund is sent
-------------------------

Where the resident paid rent electronically during the tenancy, the refund is
sent electronically, unless the resident and the office have agreed in writing
to a different method.

Where rent was not paid electronically, the refund is sent by check to the
forwarding address the resident provides. Residents should give the office a
forwarding address before moving out.

9. Questions about a specific deposit
-------------------------------------

This document describes the standard policy at Maple Court. It does not state
the deposit amount held for any particular unit, the status of any particular
refund, or what was deducted in any particular case.

A resident asking about their own deposit — the amount, the timing, or a
deduction — is directed to the resident portal or to the office.
